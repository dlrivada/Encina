## Summary

`HMACValidationPipelineBehavior` silently skipped signature validation (fail-open) whenever `HttpContext` was unavailable — a real gap for background workers or message consumers that never flow an `HttpContext`. Fixed by PR #1265 to fail closed by default, with an explicit `HttpContextRequirement` opt-out and two new regression tests. This is one of three closely-timed fail-open bugs in this sample's recent batch (with #1160/#1161), suggesting fail-open-by-accident was a systemic pattern worth a named, cited CLAUDE.md rule rather than three separate silent fixes — the main finding recommended below.
