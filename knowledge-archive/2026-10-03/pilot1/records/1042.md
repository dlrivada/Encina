## Summary

Dependabot's NuGet update job was hitting GitHub's 1-hour job timeout, blocking automated security updates. Fixed with a single-file change to `.github/dependabot.yml` (PR #1044) — most likely narrowing the update groups or schedule. Proportionate scope for a platform-configuration fix; no code, test or doc artifact was expected or needed here, and none exists. Whether the timeout actually stopped recurring can only be confirmed by watching subsequent scheduled Dependabot runs, which is outside a static-repository pilot's reach.
