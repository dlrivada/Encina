## Summary

Retention enforcement erased an entity's entire data payload once any one of its categories expired, rather than only the expired category — a serious over-erasure bug for a GDPR-adjacent compliance feature (priority-critical). Fixed together with sibling bug #1161 (`LiftHoldAsync` reporting success on failure and failing open on the other-holds check) in a single PR (#1185) that introduced a dedicated `IRetentionDataEraser` port, formalized in a new ADR-031, plus a thorough regression-test set across unit, integration and contract layers. A well-executed fix with a durable, citable destination (ADR + tests + docs), though the audit could not independently verify the Audit Trail and TimeProvider cross-cutting questions without deeper code reading.
