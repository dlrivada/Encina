## Summary

Widened the Stryker.NET mutation-testing workflow beyond a smoke test and attempted to fix coverage capture. Phase 1 (remove the break threshold) landed; Phase 2 (fix `perTest` coverage capture) was blocked by an upstream Stryker.NET/xUnit v3 bug (stryker-net#3117) and correctly spun off into a dedicated issue (#1026) rather than left dangling. The wider initiative continued through #1027 (per-folder test-case filters) and #1028 (parallel matrix execution), both also sampled in this pilot, showing a coherent multi-issue thread that is fully traceable end to end — a contrast with the early-batch issues in this same sample.
