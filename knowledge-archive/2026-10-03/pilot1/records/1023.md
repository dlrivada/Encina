## Summary

Migrated test projects across the solution to reference the `Encina.Testing.*` wrapper packages (Shouldly, Bogus, FsCheck, Verify, WireMock, Testcontainers, Fakes) instead of the underlying third-party libraries directly, and bumped outdated NuGet packages in the same pass. Delivered by PR #1024, a wide mechanical diff spanning ABAC, Secrets, Compliance and Messaging test files plus `Directory.Packages.props`. The resulting rule is now permanent CLAUDE.md guidance with a direct citation back to this issue.
