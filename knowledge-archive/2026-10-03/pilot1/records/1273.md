## Summary

`AddEncinaMongoDB` registered `InboxOrchestrator` without also registering the `InboxOptions` it depends on, so resolving it from DI would fail. Fixed by a minimal three-file PR (#1288). On its own this is a small, well-contained fix; in context with #1163 (same sample) and #1260 (seen in the broader closed-issue listing), it is the third instance in one week of a provider registering a consumer without its dependency — a pattern strong enough to justify a generic architecture test rather than three one-off fixes (see findings/proposed remediation).
