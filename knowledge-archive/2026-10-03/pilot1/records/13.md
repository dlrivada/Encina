## Summary

Proposed applying the Orchestrator pattern (as done for Validation) to the 8 caching provider packages, on the assumption of ~300-400 duplicated lines. Rejected after direct inspection showed the caching behaviors (`QueryCachingPipelineBehavior`, `CacheInvalidationPipelineBehavior`, `DistributedIdempotencyPipelineBehavior`) were already centralized in `Encina.Caching`, with providers only implementing `ICacheProvider` — no duplication existed to justify the added indirection. A well-reasoned rejection, but the reasoning lives only in the closed issue's comment, not in a citable rule.
