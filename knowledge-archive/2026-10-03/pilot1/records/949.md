## Summary

Marten's `JasperFx` projection graph validation rejected an `IServiceProvider` constructor parameter used by `Encina.Audit.Marten`'s audit projections, making them impossible to register. Fixed in PR #954 by switching to `IDocumentOperations` and constructor injection, with new unit and guard tests. The fix was generalized into a permanent CLAUDE.md rule for all event-sourced modules (citing #777, #783, #784, #785, #949 and ADR-019) — one of this sample's best examples of a bug whose lesson correctly propagated into project-wide guidance, with a citable, accurate link back to the issue number.
