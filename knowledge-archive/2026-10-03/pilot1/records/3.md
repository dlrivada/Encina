## Summary

Early technical-debt item to convert ad-hoc `ILogger.Log*` calls to `[LoggerMessage]` source-generated methods for CA1848 compliance and performance. No EventId registry existed yet at the time; that governance (central `EventIdRanges.cs`, per-package range allocation, `EventIdUniquenessRule` architecture test) was introduced ~4 months later by #1050 and enforced retroactively in September 2026 by #1126, which renumbered 754 EventIds across the whole solution. This is a clean example of a debt item whose "done" state at closing time did not match the rules the project later decided it needed.
