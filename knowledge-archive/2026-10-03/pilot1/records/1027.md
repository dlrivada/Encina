## Summary

Worked around a second upstream Stryker.NET limitation (stryker-net#3091: `--test-case-filter` only honored from the config file, not the CLI) by having each of the 17 mutation-test shards patch `stryker-config.json` with `jq` before invoking Stryker, pairing a `src/Encina/` folder with a test-namespace substring. Delivered by PR #1029. CLAUDE.md documents the mechanism and both upstream bugs it works around, but also flags its own fragility: the `FOLDERS`/`FILTERS` parallel arrays must be kept in sync by hand, with no automated check found for that invariant — a plausible small follow-up.
