## Summary

Asked to raise a single project-wide coverage threshold from an implied 45% to 85%. Closed roughly nine months later as superseded: Encina replaced the single-percentage model entirely with a per-flag obligations model (unit/guard/contract/property/integration measured independently against per-package manifest targets), which is now the only enforcement mechanism and is documented at length in CLAUDE.md. The long time this stale issue sat open after its premise became outdated is itself a process lesson.
