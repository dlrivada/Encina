## Summary

`BulkOperationsEF` still dispatched to `BulkOperationsEFOracle` and `BulkOperationsEFSqlite`, both providers ADR-009 and ADR-024 had already removed from Encina's supported matrix. Fixed by PR #1291, deleting both implementation files, updating the coverage manifest, and adding `BulkOperationsEFDispatchTests.cs`. A clean, well-scoped debt cleanup — its main interest for the pilot is as a case study in how long dead-provider references linger after a provider-removal ADR, since the two other Oracle/SQLite-adjacent issues in this same sample (#2, #21) show the same removal touching unrelated corners of the codebase at different times.
