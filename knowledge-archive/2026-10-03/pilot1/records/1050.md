## Summary

A small, correctly-scoped debt item (register one package's EventId range) turned out to be the entry point for discovering that EventId range enforcement (ADR-021) was violated across roughly 40 packages, requiring PR #1126 to renumber 754 EventIds solution-wide and land the `EventIdUniquenessRule` architecture test that now structurally prevents the whole class of bug. This is the sample's strongest example of a destination that is enforced by code (an architecture test), not just documented — the ideal outcome the pilot's audit checklist is looking for.
