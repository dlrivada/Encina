## SPEC-002 cross-check (REQ-059, minors' consent)

SPEC-002 §5 already records this exact gap: `REQ-059` ("Consent records can state that the subject is below the age of digital consent ... and that consent was given or authorised by a holder of parental authority ... the consent check refuses a minor's own consent below that age"), traceability row `REQ-059 | Consent aggregate and ConsentRequiredPipelineBehavior | Missing | no minor or parental-authority concept in src/Encina.Compliance.Consent/ | P-52`, and tracking issue **P-52** (`[FEATURE]`, P0, milestone v0.15.0 — EU Compliance: NIS2 & Digital Omnibus). This audit independently confirms the gap (no `minor`/`age`/`parental` token anywhere in `src/Encina.Compliance.Consent`, `README.md:258` states Art. 8 is "extensible" only) but does **not** duplicate P-52 as a new remediation issue — SPEC-003 §5.4 says an open issue that already covers a finding takes it as a note, not a new issue.
