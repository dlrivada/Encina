## Asked

Raise the CI line coverage threshold from 45% to 85% (with branch 80%, method 90%, no package under 80%, Core and Messaging at 90%), by adding tests and changing `.github/workflows/dotnet-ci.yml`. The 45% was a temporary reduction (commit ae9c376) and 85% came from the old CLAUDE.md testing standards. The issue rejected keeping 45%, a gradual 45-65-85 ramp and package-specific thresholds.

## Outcome

Closed not planned on 2026-09-24 with a reasoned rejection and no code change by this issue. The single global percentage no longer exists. Per-flag, per-package targets replaced it (`.github/coverage-manifest/*.json`, AGENTS.md section 9). The package-specific thresholds the issue rejected are in effect the adopted model. PR #1300 (docs, closes #1090) removed the hand-typed 85% target and other figures from README, ROADMAP and two architecture pages, and named #19, #65, #66, #67 as legacy coverage-target issues reviewed separately.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `AGENTS.md` section 9: per-flag coverage, manifest targets, no project-wide percentage, no hand-typed figures.
- `docs/testing/coverage-measurement-methodology.md`: formulas and the reason no single threshold fits (also ADR-023).
- `docs/testing/mutation-measurement-methodology.md`: per-file mutation data.
- `.github/coverage-manifest/*.json` and `.github/scripts/coverage-report.cs`: targets and computation.
- Open per-package gaps are individual `[TEST]` issues (for example #1627); #901 (low-coverage modules to 85%) is still open.
