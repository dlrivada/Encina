## Asked

Eight stream load tests (`StreamRequestLoadTests.cs`) crashed the CLR with `AccessViolationException` on .NET 10 Release builds under NBomber concurrency, inside `LanguageExt.Either.Map` / `StreamDispatcher.StreamRequestHandlerWrapper.MoveNext`. The issue asked to investigate whether this was a LanguageExt or NBomber problem and decide a fix.

## Outcome

Closed as `rejected-reasoned`: no merged PR references or closes #5 directly. Commit `e5732baa` (2025-12-22) added `[Trait("Category","Load")]` skips to the 8 tests (bundled with an unrelated dedup fix). Commit `c137e40d` (2025-12-23) recorded a root cause (dotnet/runtime#121736/PR#121771) in `ROADMAP.md` that CodeRabbit could not verify on request. The maintainer closed the issue manually on 2025-12-30, accepting the unverified citation. The affected test file was later deleted with no replacement in the Jan 2026 test consolidation (commit `65826302`).
