## Decisions
- **Deprecate `Encina.EventStoreDB`**: The team decided to remove EventStoreDB support and keep only Marten for Event Sourcing. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Retain `Encina.Marten`**: Marten was selected as the sole event sourcing backend. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Do not create `Encina.EventSourcing` abstractions package**: The decision was made to avoid a shared abstraction layer because only one provider (Marten) remains. Source: comment by dlrivada ("Answer: No").
- **Projection API differences are not a concern**: The abstraction for projections was deemed unnecessary with a single provider. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Snapshotting is a native Marten feature**: No separate strategy for snapshotting is needed. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Package count reduction**: Active packages reduced from 40 to 39. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Documentation update**: Promoted rules to `CLAUDE.md` and recorded ADRs (027-030). Source: linked PR #1113.

## Rejected alternatives
- **Option A: Strategy + Orchestrator Pattern**: Rejected due to the decision to drop one of the two strategies (EventStoreDB), making the abstraction unnecessary. Source: issue body (Option A) + comment by dlrivada.
- **Option B: Keep Both Packages Independent**: Rejected because `Encina.EventStoreDB` was deprecated. Source: issue body (Option B) + comment by dlrivada.
- **Option C: Shared Abstractions Only**: Rejected because a single provider (Marten) makes shared interfaces redundant. Source: issue body (Option C) + comment by dlrivada.

## Rules and lessons
- **Prefer single-vendor simplicity over premature abstraction**: When only one implementation is supported, avoid creating abstraction layers. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Infrastructure choice drives architecture**: Choosing Marten (PostgreSQL-based) over EventStoreDB (dedicated server) simplified infrastructure requirements. Source: comment by dlrivada.
- **Deprecation process**: Deprecate packages by moving them to a separate location, updating references, and adding deprecation notes in documentation. Source: comment by dlrivada.

## Candidate destinations

- **claude-md**: Promote the architectural preference for minimal abstraction when using single providers.
