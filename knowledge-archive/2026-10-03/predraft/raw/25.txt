## Summary

Implement snapshotting support for event-sourced aggregates with many events.

## Motivation

Aggregates with thousands of events become slow to rehydrate. Snapshotting provides periodic state saves. This is an optimization for high-volume aggregates.

## Proposed Solution

- `ISnapshotStore` abstraction
- `[Snapshot(every: 100)]` attribute
- Integration with Marten snapshot support
- Configurable snapshot frequency
