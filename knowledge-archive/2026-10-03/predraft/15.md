## Decisions
- The issue was closed as superseded by a deeper analysis indicating that Event Sourcing providers (EventStoreDB vs Marten) represent fundamentally different strategies rather than interchangeable providers, requiring a "Strategy -> Orchestrator -> Provider" pattern instead of the simple "Orchestrator + Provider" pattern (source: comment by dlrivada).
- The original proposal to centralize logic in a new `Encina.EventSourcing` package with `AggregateOrchestrator`, `IAggregateRepository`, and `IEventStore` was not executed as planned (source: comment by dlrivada).

## Rejected alternatives
- The initial approach of treating EventStoreDB and Marten as interchangeable providers under a single Orchestrator was rejected because EventStoreDB uses catch-up subscriptions/server-side projections while Marten uses inline projections/async daemons, making them distinct architectural approaches (source: comment by dlrivada).

## Rules and lessons
- When evaluating patterns for subsystems with heterogeneous backend capabilities (e.g., EventStoreDB vs Marten), consider whether they are interchangeable providers or fundamentally different strategies requiring separate orchestrators (source: comment by dlrivada).
