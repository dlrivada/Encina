- `InMemoryDistributedLockProvider` and `Encina.DistributedLock.Redis` `RedisDistributedLockProvider` wait between lock retries on the injected `TimeProvider` instead of the wall clock, so a test clock drives the whole wait and a frozen `FakeTimeProvider` no longer makes `TryAcquireAsync` spin forever (#531).
- `TryAcquireAsync` of both providers always attempts the lock once, so a zero `wait` takes a free lock instead of returning `null`, and the last retry is shortened to the remaining wait (#531).
- `InMemoryDistributedLockProvider` removes expired and released entries with compare-and-remove, so a cleanup or a late release can no longer delete a lock another caller has just acquired; its lock handles report `AcquiredAtUtc`/`ExpiresAtUtc` as `DateTimeKind.Utc` (#531).
