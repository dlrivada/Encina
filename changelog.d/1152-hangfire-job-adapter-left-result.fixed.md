- **`Encina.Hangfire` job adapters now fail the job on a handler `Left` result** (#1152). `HangfireRequestJobAdapter.ExecuteAsync` and `HangfireNotificationJobAdapter.PublishAsync` used to log a handler failure and then return normally, so Hangfire recorded the job as succeeded and never retried it. Both adapters now throw the new `EncinaJobFailedException` (carrying the `EncinaError` code) on `Left`, mirroring how `Encina.Quartz.QuartzRequestJob` throws `JobExecutionException`, so Hangfire marks the job Failed and applies its configured retry policy.
