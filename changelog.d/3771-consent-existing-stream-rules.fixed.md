- **A grant on an existing consent record now follows the renewal, regrant and reconsent rules** (#3771). `IConsentService.GrantConsentAsync` (`Encina.Compliance.Consent`) records a repeat while the consent is Active as the new `ConsentRenewedByGrant` event with its own proof. It grants a Withdrawn or Expired consent again on the same stream with the new `ConsentRegranted` event. On a consent that requires reconsent it accepts only the current version; any other version is refused with `consent.reconsent_required`, which points to `ProvideReconsentAsync`. Acts are ordered by the new optional `occurredAtUtc` parameter, the act's own timestamp (default: the injected `TimeProvider`). An act older than the consent's latest act is kept in the history without changing the state, and a withdrawal wins a tie. A timestamp more than five minutes in the future is refused with `consent.act_in_future`.

  The timestamp comes from the client application: an unsynchronised client clock can order acts inconsistently, and Encina cannot correct it. Concurrent grants on one key are retried on the reloaded stream, up to five attempts. The withdraw, renew, reconsent and invalid-transition logs no longer include the consent id. `ConsentAggregate.LastActAtUtc` and `ConsentReadModel.LastActAtUtc` are new; a read model stored before this change keeps the minimum watermark until it is rebuilt.
