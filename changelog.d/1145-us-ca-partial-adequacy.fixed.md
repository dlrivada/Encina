- `RegionRegistry.US` and `RegionRegistry.CA` no longer count as adequate for every recipient: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework only covers DPF-certified US organisations, and Canada's adequacy finding only covers PIPEDA-covered commercial organisations. `Region.RequiresRecipientCertification` flags both regions; `IAdequacyDecisionProvider.HasAdequacy`, `ICrossBorderTransferValidator.ValidateTransferAsync`, and `TransferRequest.IsRecipientCertified` (Encina.Compliance.CrossBorderTransfer) take an explicit recipient-certification confirmation, defaulting to "not adequate" without it (#1145).
