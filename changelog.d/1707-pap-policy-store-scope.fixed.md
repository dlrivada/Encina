- ABAC: the singleton persistent policy administration point no longer captures a scoped `IPolicyStore`. It opens one DI scope per operation (reads and writes), resolves the store from it and the audit store from a separate scope, and wraps the store with `CachingPolicyStoreDecorator` per scope while the cache stays shared. `PersistentPolicyAdministrationPoint` now takes `IServiceScopeFactory` as its first constructor argument instead of an `IPolicyStore`.
