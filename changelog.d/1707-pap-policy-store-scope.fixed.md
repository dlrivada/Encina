- ABAC: the singleton persistent policy administration point no longer captures a scoped `IPolicyStore`. It opens one DI scope per operation (reads and writes), resolves the store and the audit store from it, and wraps the store with `CachingPolicyStoreDecorator` per scope while the cache stays shared. `PersistentPolicyAdministrationPoint` now takes `IServiceScopeFactory` as its first constructor argument instead of an `IPolicyStore`. (#1707)
