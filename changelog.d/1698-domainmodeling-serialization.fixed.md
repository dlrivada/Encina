- **`Encina.DomainModeling` aggregates and entities round-trip through System.Text.Json** (#1698). `AggregateBase.Id`, `Entity<TId>.Id` and the audit and soft-delete setters of `AggregateRoot<TId>`, `AuditableAggregateRoot<TId>`, `SoftDeletableAggregateRoot<TId>` and `SoftDeletableEntity<TId>` are serialized with `[JsonInclude]`, so a snapshot or read model keeps its id and its audit and deletion state; `UncommittedEvents` and `DomainEvents` are no longer serialized. This applies to every System.Text.Json consumer of these base classes, not only Marten.
