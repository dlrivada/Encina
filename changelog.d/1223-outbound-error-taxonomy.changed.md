- Outbound (Refit) errors now carry an `encina.outbound.*` code and `http.status`, `http.retry_after`, `transient` metadata (plus `outbound.ambiguous` on a POST or PATCH that may already have been processed) and never the response body; `IErrorClassifier`, `ErrorClassification` and `DefaultErrorClassifier` moved from `Encina.Messaging.Recoverability` to core `Encina.Recoverability` (no alias). (#1223)
- `RetryPipelineBehavior`, the recoverability pipeline and the delayed-retry processor classify through `IErrorClassifier`, honour `Retry-After` (in-process wait up to `MaxDelayMs`, otherwise the delayed retry reschedules at that time; the outbox processor only honours `http.retry_after` as the minimum delay of `NextRetryAtUtc`, and its classification and non-idempotent rule are tracked in #2587), never retry a `Permanent` error even with `RetryOnAllExceptions`, and do not retry an ambiguous failure of a non-idempotent send unless `[Retry(RetryNonIdempotent = true)]` or an idempotency key opts in; HTTP 408 and connection-pool exhaustion are now transient. (#1223)
