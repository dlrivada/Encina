- Pipeline stage contract vocabulary (#2184, phase 1): `PipelineStage` (16 stages), `PipelineStageAttribute`, `PipelineStepDescription` with `PipelineStepKind` and `PipelineStepSource`, `IEncinaPipelineInspector`, and the new `IRequestErrorProcessor<,>` and `IRequestPostCommitProcessor<,>` contracts. `IRequestPreProcessor<>.Process` now returns `ValueTask<Either<EncinaError, Unit>>` (a `Left` aborts the request) and `IRequestPostProcessor<,>.Process` receives the successful `TResponse`, runs only on success and its `Left` fails the request. Pipeline order does not change yet.
