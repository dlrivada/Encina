- `DPIAOptions.EnableExpirationMonitoring` is removed: the DPIA review-reminder service can no longer be switched off, because the periodic review that GDPR Article 35(11) requires is a legal obligation (#3270, #3786). The `expirationMonitoringEnabled` entry of the DPIA health-check data and the "DPIA review reminder service is disabled" log message (EventId 8841) are removed with it.
