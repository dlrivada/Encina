- `DelayedRetryProcessor` no longer warns and skips, or falls back, when a dependency is missing: `IEncina`, `IDelayedRetryScheduler` and `IMessageSerializer` are required. With delayed retries on, a missing `IEncina` fails the host start through the delayed retry startup check (naming `IEncina`) and, without a host, fails the processing cycle with the `ProcessingError` log; the scheduler registration still does not require `IEncina`. EventIds 2849 and 2856 are retired (#2918).
