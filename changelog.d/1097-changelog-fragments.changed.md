- **Changelog entries and `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt` appends no longer conflict across parallel PRs** (#1097). Changelog entries move to one file per change under `changelog.d/<issue>-<slug>.<section>.md` (see `changelog.d/README.md`); `changelog-fragments.cs` validates fragments in CI (`--check`), previews the merged `[Unreleased]` section (`--preview`) and folds fragments into a dated release section at release time (`--release <version> <date> [title]`), then deletes the folded files. `PublicAPI.Unshipped.txt` gains `merge=union` in `.gitattributes` so concurrent appends merge cleanly during local merges and rebases instead of conflicting.
