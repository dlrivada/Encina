- **ABAC decision audit observability** (#751). The decision audit emits counters `abac.decision_audit.recorded` (tags `abac.outcome`, `abac.enforcement_mode`) and `abac.decision_audit.failed` (tags `abac.failure_mode`, `error.type`), the histogram `abac.decision_audit.duration` (milliseconds), and the span `ABAC.DecisionAudit.Record`, a child of `ABAC.Evaluate`, which also carries the tag `abac.decision_id`. Tags carry codes and exception type names only, never messages.
