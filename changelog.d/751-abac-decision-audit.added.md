- **ABAC decision audit** (#751). `Encina.Security.ABAC` can now audit every decision its Policy Enforcement Point enforces. It is opt-in and off by default: enable it with `ABACOptions.DecisionAudit.Enabled` or `ABACOptions.AuditDecisions(...)`. Each decision is written before the request proceeds, through the application's `IOperationAuditStore`, as an `OperationAuditEntry` with Action `ABACDecision`. It is fail-closed by default (`ABACDecisionAuditFailureMode.FailClosed`, bounded by `WriteTimeout`); `BestEffort` is an explicit, logged opt-out. `IABACDecisionAuditReader` reads the decisions back with a typed reader and exports them as JSON Lines. The decision audit also adds a health state, metrics and EventIds 9079-9090 (see ADR-047).
