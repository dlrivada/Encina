- An authenticated principal whose subject claim is missing, malformed, reserved (the `service:` prefix) or conflicting is now refused instead of opening an anonymous scope: `RunAsPrincipalAsync`, `RunInboundAsync` and `RunHostInboundAsync` return `Left` with `encina.identity.missing_subject_claim`, `encina.identity.malformed_subject_claim`, `encina.identity.reserved_subject_claim` or `encina.identity.conflicting_subject_claims` (new `RequestIdentityErrorCodes` constants and `RequestIdentityErrors` factories) and never invoke the work delegate; an unauthenticated principal still opens the anonymous scope (#2855).
- `UseEncinaContext()` answers such a request, and the existing inbound tenant conflict, with 403 `application/problem+json` and a fixed detail without claim values, without running the rest of the pipeline; there is no opt-out, so an authenticated principal without a subject is also refused on `[AllowAnonymous]` endpoints (#2855).
