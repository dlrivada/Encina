- Transactional outbox on all 10 database providers (ADO.NET, Dapper and EF Core on SqlServer, PostgreSQL and MySQL, plus MongoDB): the outbox rows of a request's `IHasNotifications` notifications are written inside the request's single transaction, so a business change and its events commit or roll back together; a failed outbox write or commit fails the request instead of losing or phantom-publishing events (#718).
- The transactional outbox is the only outbox write path; the `OutboxPostProcessor` that wrote after the transaction no longer exists (#718).
