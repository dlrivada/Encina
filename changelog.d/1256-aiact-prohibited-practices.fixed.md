- `ProhibitedPractice` in `Encina.Compliance.AIAct` now has a member for every point of AI Act Art. 5(1) as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: `VulnerabilityExploitation` (5(1)(b)), `NonConsensualIntimateDeepfakes` (5(1)(ba)) and `ChildSexualAbuseMaterial` (5(1)(bb)); the last two apply from 2 December 2026. `ProhibitedPracticeExtensions.ArticlePoint` returns each member's Art. 5(1) point. (#1256)
- The `Encina.Compliance.AIAct` README, XML docs and prohibited-use log message no longer claim prohibited practices are blocked "unconditionally": every practice declared on a registered AI system is blocked in Block and Warn mode, and Disabled mode skips all checks. (#1256)
