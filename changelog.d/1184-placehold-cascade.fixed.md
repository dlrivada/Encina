- **`PlaceHoldAsync` reports placement failures and fails closed** (#1184). `ILegalHoldService.PlaceHoldAsync` ignored the result of cascading each legal hold to retention records and swallowed exceptions, so a record could fail to be held while the call reported success. Now every record is attempted, the ones that fail stay unprotected and the call returns `retention.hold_placement_incomplete` with their ids under `failedRecordIds`; a later `PlaceHoldAsync` call for the same entity retries only the records not yet held, mirroring the existing `LiftHoldAsync` design for consistency.
