- Dapper outbox (SqlServer, PostgreSQL, MySQL): the outbox rows of a request's `IHasNotifications` notifications are now written inside the request's single database transaction, so a business change and its events commit or roll back together; a failed outbox write or commit fails the request, and `UnitOfWorkDapper` shares that transaction and no longer begins, commits or rolls back synchronously (#719, #1418).
- On Dapper SqlServer and MySQL, components that do not yet join the request transaction (repositories other than the unit of work's, bulk operations, dead letter, audit, ABAC and anonymization stores) are rejected inside the transaction the outbox now opens for IHasNotifications requests (#1934).
