- **Declared service identities** (#1705). `services.AddEncinaServiceIdentity(name, id => id.WithRoles(...).WithPermissions(...).WithClaim(...))` declares a least-privilege identity (subject `service:<name>`), validated at startup (name pattern, no wildcards, `encina.` reserved for built-in identities, conflicting redeclarations fail); `IServiceIdentityCatalog` reads the declarations.
- **Delegate-only identity scopes** (#1705). `IRequestContextScopeFactory` (`RunAsServiceAsync`, `RunAsPrincipalAsync`, `RunInboundAsync`, `RunRestoredAsync`, plus `Task` overloads) is the only way to bind an identity to a unit of work. Scopes return `Either`, never invoke the work on a refusal, end when the work completes, and refuse to open over a user identity or an inbound request (`IdentityScopeOptions.AllowOverInbound` opts in for service and principal scopes, logged). `RunRestoredAsync` rebuilds the originating actor of a deferred message from `PersistedRequestIdentity` and the required `PersistedIdentitySource` (external messages run anonymous with only the dispatcher's trusted tenant). `InboundRequestInfo` carries what an entry point read from its caller and is normalized, never refused.
- **Identity hardening** (#1705). An explicit context passed to `Send`, `Publish` or `Stream` must carry a live identity issued by a scope (new error codes `encina.identity.tenant_conflict`, `unknown_service_identity`, `reserved_service_identity`, `unsupported_accessor`, `invalid_persisted_identity`); the `IRequestContextAccessor` setter only preserves the identity and origin it finds and never clears; identity comparison ignores the per-token claims of `RequestIdentityOptions.PerTokenClaimTypes`; `RequestIdentity.Principal` returns a copy of the authenticated identities only; a custom `IRequestContextAccessor` fails startup. EventIds 166-175 log every scope opening, refusal and ending without user ids, roles, tenants or claim values.
- **Test builders** (#1705). `TestIdentity.Service(...)` and `TestIdentity.Principal(...)` in `Encina.Testing` build identities and principals for tests; binding them goes through the scope factory.
