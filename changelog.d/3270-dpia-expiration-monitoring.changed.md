- `AddEncinaDPIA` always registers the DPIA review-reminder hosted service (with `TryAddEnumerable`, so once however often it is called). It runs a first cycle at start and then one every `DPIAOptions.ExpirationCheckInterval` (default 1 hour) on the injected `TimeProvider`. The interval is now always validated and must be between 1 millisecond and `uint.MaxValue - 1` milliseconds (about 49.7 days), the range `PeriodicTimer` supports. The review-reminder log messages moved from EventIds 8842-8848 to 8841-8847 (#3786).
