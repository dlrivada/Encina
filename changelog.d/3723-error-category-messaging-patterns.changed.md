- Every error of the content router, routing slip, saga and scatter-gather producers (`Encina.Messaging`) now declares an `ErrorCategory` (ADR-062, #3723); the code strings and messages are unchanged.
- HTTP status changes: the nine `*.cancelled` codes (`content_router.cancelled` x2, `routingslip.handler.cancelled`, `saga.handler.cancelled` x2, `scattergather.cancelled` x4) answer `Cancelled` (499) instead of 500; `saga.invalid_status` (5 calls) answers `Conflict` (409) instead of 500; `scattergather.timeout` answers `Timeout` (504) instead of 500; `dlq.source_message_id_required` answers `InvalidRequest` (400) instead of 500. `saga.not_found` stays `NotFound` (404) and `content_router.no_matching_route` stays `Internal` (500).
- `scattergather.quorum_not_reached` and `scattergather.all_scatters_failed` take a derived category: the category shared by the errors of the failed scatter handlers (for example a 404, 403, 502 or 503 when every failed handler answered that), `Internal` (500) when the categories are mixed or absent, and `Timeout` (504) when the operation's time budget cut the fan-out; before they always answered 500.
