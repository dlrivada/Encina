- `Encina.Security.PII`: a `[PII]` or `[SensitiveData]` property that is not a `string` (for example `int`, `Guid`, a collection or a nested object) no longer escapes masking silently; masking now fails closed with `InvalidOperationException` in the response, audit and log contexts, whatever the value, and the failure surfaces as `pii.masking_failed`, `audit.masking_failed` or the `[MASKING FAILED]` log placeholder (#3032).
