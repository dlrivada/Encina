- The 43 error creations of `Encina.Dapper.SqlServer`, `Encina.Dapper.PostgreSQL` and `Encina.Dapper.MySQL` now declare an `ErrorCategory` (#3729, ADR-062); the error codes and messages are unchanged.
- `encina.sharding.all_replicas_unhealthy` (3 calls, one per provider, from the Dapper `ShardedReadWriteConnectionFactory` when `FallbackToPrimaryWhenNoReplicas` is off and every replica is marked unhealthy) is `ErrorCategory.Unavailable` and now answers HTTP 503 instead of 500, with no `Retry-After`, and gRPC and GraphQL report `UNAVAILABLE`; the other 40 calls are `ErrorCategory.Internal` and keep answering 500.
