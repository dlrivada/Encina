- Recoverability is configured through the options pattern: `AddEncinaRecoverability` and `UseRecoverability` both configure one `RecoverabilityOptions` read through `IOptions<RecoverabilityOptions>`; the later registration wins as a whole, so the two can no longer disagree about delayed retries (`RecoverabilityPipelineBehavior` and `DelayedRetryProcessor` now take `IOptions<RecoverabilityOptions>`) (#3262).
