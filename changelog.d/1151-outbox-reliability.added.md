- **Outbox retry, exhaustion and requeue APIs** (#1150, #1151). `OutboxProcessorBase` in `Encina.Messaging` is the shared background processor; `OutboxRetryBackoff` computes the capped, jittered delay. `IOutboxStore` gains `GetPendingCountAsync`, `GetExhaustedCountAsync` and `RequeueExhaustedAsync`, implemented by the ADO.NET and Dapper stores for SQL Server, PostgreSQL and MySQL, the EF Core store, the MongoDB store and the `Encina.Testing.Fakes` store. `OutboxOrchestrator` exposes `GetExhaustedCountAsync` and two `RequeueExhaustedAsync` overloads (all, or by message id); a requeue resets `RetryCount`, `NextRetryAtUtc` and `ErrorMessage` and is logged with EventId 2959.
- **Outbox options for backoff** (#1150). `OutboxOptions.MaxRetryDelay` (default 10 minutes) caps the retry delay and `OutboxOptions.RetryJitterRatio` (default 0.2) randomises it.
- **Outbox metrics** (#1150, #1151). `encina.outbox.processor.messages_total` counts messages by `outcome` (`success`, `failure`, `exhausted`, `store_error`, `unsaved`), recorded once the batch is saved; `encina.outbox.messages_requeued_total` counts requeued messages; the `encina.outbox.messages_exhausted` gauge reports the last observed number of exhausted messages.
- **Outbox health thresholds for exhausted messages** (#1150). `OutboxHealthCheckOptions.ExhaustedMessageWarningThreshold` and `ExhaustedMessageCriticalThreshold` (defaults 1 and 100) make `OutboxHealthCheck` report Degraded or Unhealthy when messages have used up their retries.
- **Outbox log events** (#1151). EventIds 2960 (batch save failed), 2961 (store failed to record a message outcome) and 2962 (batch stopped by cancellation) in `MessagingLog`.
