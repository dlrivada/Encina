- **`ABACDecisionAuditOptions.AllowCrossTenantQueries` removed** (#3271). The ABAC decision audit reader can no longer be switched into a cross-tenant mode: in a multi-tenant application (`MultiTenancyMarker` registered) a query or export without an ambient tenant always returns `encina.authorization.abac_audit_tenant_required`, with no opt-out, and the cross-tenant warning (EventId 9090) is gone. Single-tenant applications are unchanged.
