- **ABAC decision trace, phase 1** (#751). `PolicyEvaluationContext.IncludeEvaluationTrace` makes the policy decision point return an evaluation trace (`PolicyDecision.EvaluatedPolicies`, `PolicyEvaluationTrace`, `PolicyTraceReason`) and the representative decisive `PolicyDecision.RuleId`; the trace is bounded by `PolicyEvaluationContext.MaxTraceEntries` and costs nothing when off.
- **ABAC decision audit models, phase 1** (#751). New `Encina.Security.ABAC.DecisionAudit` models (`ABACDecisionRecord`, `ABACEnforcedOutcome`, `IABACResourceIdentity`, `ABACDecisionAuditSchema`) and the errors `ABACErrors.DecisionAuditFailed`, `InvalidDecisionAuditQuery`, `DecisionAuditStoreUnavailable`, `DecisionAuditTenantRequired` and `DecisionAuditTenantMismatch`; the decision audit itself arrives in later phases.
