- **Retention enforcement now completes the record lifecycle** (#1142). Each expired record moves `Active → Expired` before erasure and `→ Deleted` only after a successful erasure. The results of `EraseAsync`, `MarkExpiredAsync` and `MarkDeletedAsync` are checked: a failure (including an erasure that reports failed fields) is logged and counted in `RecordsFailedTotal`, never as a deletion. `GetExpiredRecordsAsync` also returns `Expired` records, so a record whose erasure or deletion failed is retried on the next cycle, while `Deleted` records are never selected again and their entities are not erased twice.
- **Retention enforcement fails closed when the legal-hold lookup fails** (#1143). If `ILegalHoldService.HasActiveHoldsAsync` returns an error, the record is skipped without erasure, logged (EventId 8586) and counted as failed, and the next cycle retries it.
- **Encina.Compliance.Retention takes time from `TimeProvider`** (#1146). `RetentionRecordAggregate` decides between `Active` and `Expired` after a hold release from the release event's `OccurredAtUtc`, so replaying the stream is deterministic; `RetentionEnforcementService` and `RetentionValidationPipelineBehavior` accept an optional `TimeProvider` (defaulting to `TimeProvider.System`) for the expiry alert window and the logged expiry.
