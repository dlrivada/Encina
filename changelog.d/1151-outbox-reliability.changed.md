- **Breaking: `OutboxOrchestrator.ProcessPendingMessagesAsync` takes a callback that reports the delivery result** (#1151). The callback returns `ValueTask<Either<EncinaError, Unit>>`; a `Left` is handled like a thrown exception.
- **Breaking: `IOutboxStore` has three new members** (#1150). Custom stores must implement `GetPendingCountAsync`, `GetExhaustedCountAsync` and `RequeueExhaustedAsync`.
- **Breaking: `OutboxHealthCheck` takes `OutboxOptions`** (#1150). The constructor is `OutboxHealthCheck(IOutboxStore, OutboxOptions, OutboxHealthCheckOptions?)`, so that pending and exhausted messages are counted against the configured `MaxRetries`.
- **Breaking: `OutboxOptions` validates its values** (#1151). `MaxRetries` and `BatchSize` must be at least 1, `BaseRetryDelay` greater than zero and `RetryJitterRatio` between 0 and 1 (the setters throw `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`); `MaxRetryDelay` must be at least `BaseRetryDelay` (the outbox processor and `OutboxOrchestrator` throw `ArgumentException` when created). `MaxRetries` is documented as the maximum number of delivery attempts, the first one included.
- **The EF Core outbox processor honours `OutboxOptions.EnableProcessor`** (#1151). It derives from `OutboxProcessorBase` like the ADO.NET and Dapper processors and logs with the shared `MessagingLog` EventIds.
- **Outbox EventIds retired** (#1151). The EF Core processor's EventIds 3026-3034 and the `OutboxOrchestrator` per-message EventIds 2838-2841 are no longer written; message processing logs with EventIds 2830-2833 and 2958-2962.
- **Breaking: the outbox health check is no longer tagged `ready` by default** (#1151). `OutboxHealthCheck` and `AddEncinaOutbox` report Degraded/Unhealthy for backlog or exhausted messages but no longer affect readiness probes out of the box; register the check with the `ready` tag explicitly to restore the previous behaviour.
- **Breaking: `OutboxOptions` retry defaults changed** (#1151). `MaxRetries` is now 10 (was 3), `BaseRetryDelay` is 30 seconds (was 5 seconds) and `MaxRetryDelay` is 1 hour (was 10 minutes), giving messages more time and attempts to recover from transient failures.
