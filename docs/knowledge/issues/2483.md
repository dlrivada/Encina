## Asked

Make an issue labelled `urgent` rank above every non-urgent issue in the Priority list, by adding 100 to its computed total.

## Outcome

`Get-PriorityUrgentBonus` in `tools/ai/priority/_priority-lib.ps1` returns 100 for the label `urgent` (case-insensitive); `New-ScoredEntry` in `score-issues.ps1` adds it, keeps `baseTotal`, `urgent` and the `urgent` flag, and recomputes urgency from the labels on every run including `-Resume`. SPEC-004 REQ-010 and the open-issue skill describe the rule and the 24 h freshness requirement.
