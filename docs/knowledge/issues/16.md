## Asked

A time-boxed spike to evaluate a Strategy pattern unifying Encina's two Saga approaches, Orchestration and Choreography, behind one interface.

## Outcome

Delivered without a pull request: the maintainer rejected unifying the two patterns and kept them separate, distinct systems, application-wide selection, no automatic migration between them. The deliverable was documentation, added directly by commit `8301a07c` (`docs/messaging/sagas.md`, `docs/messaging/index.md`).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/messaging/sagas.md`, `docs/messaging/index.md` — present today; decision flowchart, comparison table and FAQ.
- `docs/architecture/adr/` — missing; the decision lives only as prose in `sagas.md` (remediation draft `16-docs-5`).
- `src/Encina.Messaging/Sagas/` and `src/Encina.Messaging/Choreography/` — still separate folders, no shared interface, confirming the decision holds in code.
