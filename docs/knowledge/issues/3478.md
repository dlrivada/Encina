## Asked

Remove the dead Codecov references from the PR watcher agent, `watch-open-prs.ps1`, the GraphQL-down self-test and the lychee configuration, now that the Codecov service is gone.

## Outcome

Delivered: the `codecov/patch` check, the `codecov[bot]` bot, the patch percentage and the Codecov known non-failure left `pr-watcher.md`; the exclusion left `watch-open-prs.ps1`; the self-test fixture uses `ci/external`. In `.github/lychee.toml` the `codecov.io` badge exclusion was removed (the badge left the pages in #3479); the `docs.codecov.com` exclusion stays because ADR-023 keeps its links as the historical record. The `codecov[bot]` entries in the author lists of `fetch-closed-issue-data.ps1`, `historian-extract.ps1` and `historian-extract-closed.ps1` are harmless historical-author lists and stay.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/agents/pr-watcher.md` — the relevant checks and bots as they really are.
- `tools/ai/watch-open-prs.ps1` — reports every failed check.
- `tools/ai/gh-graphql-down-selftest.ps1` — failed commit statuses are reported.
- `.github/lychee.toml` — the remaining `docs.codecov.com` exclusion and why.
