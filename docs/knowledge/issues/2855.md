## Asked

Stop `RunAsPrincipalAsync` and `RunInboundAsync` from opening an Anonymous scope for an authenticated principal with a missing, malformed, reserved or conflicting subject, and refuse it fail closed instead (AGENTS.md section 3, SPEC-002 DEC-006).

## Outcome

Both paths call `CheckSubject` and return one of four codes before opening the scope, so the work delegate never runs, and the middleware answers 403 for them. `MapErrorCodeToStatusCode` still maps the `encina.identity.*` refusal codes to 500; a follow-up issue was drafted.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Identity/RequestContextScopeFactory.cs` — the subject check in `CheckPrincipalScope` and `CheckInbound`.
- `src/Encina/Identity/ClaimsRequestIdentityFactory.cs` — `CheckSubject` and the subject resolver.
- `src/Encina/Identity/RequestIdentityErrors.cs` — the four subject errors and their codes.
- `src/Encina.AspNetCore/EncinaContextMiddleware.cs` — the 403 answer.
- `src/Encina.AspNetCore/README.md` — the subject refusal table.
