## Asked

Remove the CA1830 warning that the SDK 11 analyzers report in `DateOfBirthMaskingStrategy`, with a change that is valid on SDK 10 and on SDK 11 / net11.0 and does not alter the masked output.

## Outcome

`result.Append(new string(options.MaskCharacter, parts[i].Length))` became `result.Append(options.MaskCharacter, parts[i].Length)`. The package builds with zero warnings on SDK 10, and CA1830 is absent on SDK 11 for net11.0. No suppression was added.

- `src/Encina.Security.PII/Strategies/DateOfBirthMaskingStrategy.cs` — the corrected call.
