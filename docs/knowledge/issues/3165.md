## Asked

Make the purge settings of `InboxOptions` reject values the inbox purge service (#3013, parent #1969) cannot run with: a non-positive or too large `PurgeInterval`, and a non-positive `PurgeBatchSize`. The issue landed before #3013 so the service can rely on valid options.

## Outcome

Delivered: the setters of `PurgeInterval`, `PurgeBatchSize` and `MessageRetentionPeriod` throw `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` for out-of-range values and keep the previous value. `PurgeInterval` must be greater than zero and at most `uint.MaxValue - 1` milliseconds (about 49.7 days, the longest delay `Task.Delay` accepts); `PurgeBatchSize` must be at least 1 and has no ceiling because the stores chunk internally (decision D14 of #1969).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Messaging/Inbox/InboxOptions.cs` - validated setters and their XML documentation.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/Purge/InboxOptionsValidationTests.cs` - bounds, exactly the maximum and one tick above it.
- `tests/Encina.GuardTests/Messaging/Inbox/InboxOptionsPurgeGuardTests.cs` - the property name and the previous value kept after a rejected set.
