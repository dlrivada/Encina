## Asked

Make the closed-issue audit output permanent: publish each audit's record, result and stage files into `docs/knowledge/` through a pull request, let the validator accept and check them, and backfill audits #1 to #29 from the backup branch.

## Outcome

Delivered: `audit-done.ps1` publishes through `Publish-AuditKnowledge` in `_audit-lib.ps1` (temporary worktree, branch `knowledge/audit-<n>`, validation of the whole tree, commit, push, `gh pr create` with `Refs #1345`) and keeps the audit worktree, branch and `current-audit.json` when publishing fails; `-NoPublish` prepares the branch and shows the commands. `knowledge-records.cs` gained schema 2, the `audit.record` existence rule and the audits-folder rule, with `knowledge-records-selftest.ps1`; `audit-done-selftest.ps1` runs the real script against a fixture with stubbed git and gh. Audits #1 to #29 were backfilled (22 records of schema 1 and 2, 22 results of which #27, #28 and #29 were generated, and 13 stage folders for #16 to #29 except #20); nine existing records that named a result file that was never written now say `not written yet` (#1345 also went from `conforms` to `not-audited`, since no audit ran). `audit-done.ps1` now refuses to publish until the remediation drafts are opened (`open-remediation.ps1` runs first), sets the published record's `audit.verdict` and `audit.record`, and `audit.record` must be exactly `docs/knowledge/audits/issue-<n>.md`.

Eight backfilled files differ from `knowledge-archive/2026-10-03` on `origin/knowledge/audit-archive`: `issues/1.md`, `issues/3.md`, `issues/6.md` and `issues/9.md` (the `audit.record` line pointed into the git-ignored `artifacts/`), and `audits/issue-2.md`, `audits/issue-8.md`, `audits/issue-9.md`, `issues/5.md` and `issues/9.md` (relative links to `docs/` rewritten to `../../`; `issues/9.md` has both changes). That is 8 distinct files plus the generated results `audits/issue-27.md`, `issue-28.md` and `issue-29.md`, which do not exist in the archive. The six pilot-format records (#11 to #15, #20) and audit #4 are not backfilled.
