## Asked

Deliver the HTTP column of ADR-062 once, in core `Encina`, as data: the status of each `ErrorCategory`, the reason phrases and the `Retry-After` and `WWW-Authenticate` rules, so the three REST hosts can delete their copies (#3688, #3689, #3690). No host or producer changes.

## Outcome

`ErrorCategoryHttp` (`src/Encina/Errors/ErrorCategoryHttp.cs`) with unit and property tests, per-file coverage targets, and the ADR and pending-file corrections from H1-H4 to H1-H3.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Errors/ErrorCategoryHttp.cs` - the table, the reason phrases and the header rules.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract and the staged migration.
