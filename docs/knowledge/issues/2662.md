## Asked

Reject an unsupported `HashChainOptions.HashAlgorithm` at construction and at startup instead of failing on the first attestation, and keep the HMAC key out of serialization and `ToString()`.

## Outcome

`GetRecommendedKeySize` no longer falls back to 32 bytes; the provider constructor and a new options validator reject unsupported algorithms; `HmacKey` is excluded from JSON and redacted in `ToString()`.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Compliance.Attestation/ContentHasher.cs` - the supported set.
- `src/Encina.Compliance.Attestation/Validation/HashChainOptionsValidator.cs` - the startup check.
- `src/Encina.Compliance.Attestation/HashChainOptions.cs` - the redaction.
