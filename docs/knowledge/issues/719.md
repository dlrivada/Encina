## Asked

Make the outbox rows of a request and its business change commit or roll back together on the three Dapper providers, as PR #2558 did for ADO.NET: Phase 6 of the outbox atomicity plan (#718).

## Outcome

Delivered: `AddEncinaDapper` registers the transactional outbox behavior on SqlServer, PostgreSQL and MySQL, the four messaging stores enlist in the scope's shared transaction, and `UnitOfWorkDapper` leases that transaction. Real-database tests cover commit, `Left`, exception, failed outbox write and the shared unit of work on all three.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Dapper.SqlServer/ServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` (and the PostgreSQL and MySQL twins) - the write path and the accessor registration.
- `src/Encina.Dapper.SqlServer/UnitOfWork/UnitOfWorkDapper.cs` - the lease-based unit of work.
- `docs/architecture/adr/037-transactional-outbox.md` - provider status and the design.
- `docs/features/transactional-outbox.md` - how to write the outbox inside the request's transaction.
