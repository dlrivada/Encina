## Asked

Enforce template compliance at agent spawn time: many open issues lacked their template headers (the issue cites an audit export of 2026-10-06) and nothing stopped a worker from starting on one.

## Outcome

A PreToolUse hook on the Agent tool, `check-target-issue-template.ps1`, reads the `Issue #<n>` that opens the first line of an `issue-worker` or `docs-writer` brief, loads the issue with `gh`, picks the template from the title prefix and denies the spawn unless every level-2 header of that template appears verbatim and in order. It fails closed (no issue number, a `gh` failure, an unknown prefix, an unreadable template, a missing helper). The header extraction and the template map moved to `_issue-templates.ps1`, shared with `check-issue-template.ps1`.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/check-target-issue-template.ps1` — the spawn gate.
- `.claude/hooks/_issue-templates.ps1` — the shared template and header helpers.
- `.claude/skills/worker-brief/SKILL.md` — step 1 states the rewrite-before-brief rule.
