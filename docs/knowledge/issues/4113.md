## Asked

Move the six EventId ranges of module isolation, the four messaging stores and IdGeneration into the 10000+ zone with every literal id and citation, in one pull request and with no change of behavior.

## Outcome

The six ranges and their 49 literal ids moved together (a range is an atom for `EncinaEventIdAllocationTests` and `EventIdUniquenessRule`). Source comments, the Messaging README, the tests that cite purge ids and the registry cite the new numbers; plans and knowledge records keep the old ones and the registry's migration map translates them.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Diagnostics/EventIdRanges.cs` — the registered ranges.
- `docs/engineering/eventid-block-registry.md` — registered rows, block status and the migration map.
