## Asked

Let the SPEC-003 audit pipeline run 2 to 5 audits at once instead of one, each audit still individual (its own worktree, agents, phases, verifier loop and pull request), with the hooks enforcing order and ownership per audit and the result identical to a sequential run.

## Outcome

Per-audit state files with a resolver, the `maxParallelAudits` setting, the scope-overlap rule in `audit-next.ps1`, the cross-audit duplicate check in `-Prepare`, `open-remediation.ps1` and `audit-verifier`, per-audit guard and ownership in both hooks, the migration of the old `current-audit.json`, the board reconciler reading every open audit, and a new self-test (`audit-parallel-selftest.ps1`) plus `Test-Hooks.ps1` cases for two and three concurrent audits.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/audit/_audit-lib.ps1` - open-audit state, resolver, scope and overlap, concurrent drafts.
- `tools/ai/audit/audit-next.ps1` - limit, overlap rule, queue order.
- `.claude/hooks/audit-stage-guard.ps1`, `.claude/hooks/enforce-path-ownership.ps1`, `.claude/hooks/_open-audits.ps1` - per-audit order and ownership.
- `.claude/skills/issue-audit/SKILL.md` - the orchestrator's procedure for parallel audits.
