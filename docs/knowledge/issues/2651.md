## Asked

Raise the unit flag of `Encina.Testing.WireMock` from 20.63 percent to at least 60 percent and the guard flag from 12.03 percent to at least 15 percent, because the behaviour tests for stubs, faults, sequences, the Refit fixture and the webhook extensions lived only in the integration project.

## Outcome

Delivered with new tests only. The unit project gained behaviour tests for `EncinaWireMockFixture`, `EncinaRefitMockFixture<T>` and `WebhookTestingExtensions` (every branch of `GetReceivedWebhookBodies`) plus the pre-initialization behaviour of `WireMockContainerFixture`; the guard project gained guards for the Refit fixture, `VerifyCallMade`, `VerifyNoCallsMade` and `CreateClient(handler)`. Measured with `coverage-report.cs`, the unit flag reached 95 percent and the guard flag 23 percent. No `src/` change was needed and no manifest target was changed.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Testing/WireMock/` — the in-process behaviour tests.
- `tests/Encina.GuardTests/Testing/WireMock/WireMockFixtureGuardTests.cs` — the added guard clauses.
