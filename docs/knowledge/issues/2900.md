## Asked

Make the EPIC #2845 rule "a worker gets a ready sub-issue of one worker PR" checkable: the spawn hook refuses a brief for `issue-worker` or `docs-writer` that does not declare `Size: <files> files, <lines> lines` within 15 files and 1500 lines, denies `[EPIC]` targets and applies the issue-path-count checks; the worker-brief skill, the agents README and adversarial-reviewer record the rule.

## Outcome

Delivered in `check-target-issue-template.ps1` (two constants, the EPIC, Size and path-count checks after the template check), with new cases in `Test-Hooks.ps1` and the rule recorded in the worker-brief skill, `.claude/agents/README.md` and `adversarial-reviewer.md`.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/check-target-issue-template.ps1` - the size gate and its limits.
- `.claude/skills/worker-brief/SKILL.md` - the Size rule (section 1), the `Size:` line of the fixed part and the decompose-first note.
- `.claude/agents/adversarial-reviewer.md` - the review-time comparison of `git diff --shortstat` with the declaration.
