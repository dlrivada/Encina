## Asked

Make the replication loop of `PostgresCdcConnector` (change-event mapping and error paths) unit testable without a live PostgreSQL server, with no behaviour change, then raise the connector's unit target.

## Outcome

The Npgsql calls (current WAL LSN, ensure publication, replication stream) now sit behind an internal interface `IPostgresReplicationClient` implemented by `NpgsqlReplicationClient`; the stream yields decoded internal records (relation, insert, update, delete, skipped, decode failure) instead of Npgsql message types, which have no public constructors. `PostgresCdcConnector` keeps position restore, the `CreatePublicationIfNotExists` and `CreateSlotIfNotExists` flags, the mapping to `ChangeEvent` with `TimeProvider`, and the error mapping to `CdcErrors`. The client is registered with `TryAddSingleton` in `AddEncinaCdcPostgreSql` so an application or test can replace it. Unit tests drive the connector through a hand-written fake of the interface. The known defects #2776, #2777, #2778 and #2779 are deliberately unchanged. The figures live in the coverage manifest and on the coverage dashboard.
