## Asked

Stop `PIIPropertyScanner` from skipping `[PII]` and `[SensitiveData]` properties that are not `string`: a decorated value the masker cannot mask must not reach a response, an audit entry or a log in clear.

## Outcome

A non-string property with `[PII]` or `[SensitiveData]` is kept by the scanner with `Unmaskable = true` and `LogOnly = false` (precedence as for strings: `[PII]`, `[SensitiveData]`, `[MaskInLogs]`). `PIIMasker` no longer filters it out of the response and audit contexts, so `SkipsContext` throws `InvalidOperationException` in every context, whatever the value. The pipeline returns `pii.masking_failed`, audit returns `audit.masking_failed` and `LogMasked` writes `[MASKING FAILED]`. Class-level attributes still cover string properties only; nested, collection and interface-typed members fail closed until #1834 masks them.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Security.PII/Internal/PIIPropertyScanner.cs` — `FromUnmaskable` and the precedence.
- `src/Encina.Security.PII/PIIMasker.cs` — `ScanProperties`, `SkipsContext` and the message.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Security/PII/PIINonStringFailClosedTests.cs` — the per-context regression tests.
- `.github/coverage-manifest/Encina.Security.PII.json` — the per-flag targets and justifications.
