## Asked

Sub-issue S1 of #3419: add the error category contract to core `Encina` (the `ErrorCategory` enum, the categorized `EncinaErrors.Create` and `FromException` overloads, `GetCategory()`, `EncinaErrorMetadataKeys`), the transitional `LegacyErrorCategoryInference`, the completeness scan with its pending list, and ADR-062, without changing any host or producer package. It unblocks #1922.

## Outcome

`src/Encina/Errors/ErrorCategory.cs` defines the 14 categories with stable values. `EncinaErrors.Create(code, category, message, ...)` and `FromException(code, category, exception, ...)` store the category on the internal carrier; `GetCategory()` returns it, `Internal` for a code-less error, and otherwise the inference. The built-in `Unknown`, `Forbidden`, `Unauthorized` and `Unauthenticated` factories declare their category. `ErrorCategoryCompletenessStaticScanTests` scans `src/`; `ErrorCategoryPending.txt` was generated by one scan run and lists every uncategorized path prefix with its slice or owning issue. ADR-062 records the HTTP, gRPC and GraphQL columns, rules R1-R7, the header rules and the F2 end state.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` — the contract, the tables, the rules and the migration.
- `src/Encina/Results/EncinaErrors.cs` — the overloads and `GetCategory`.
- `src/Encina/Errors/LegacyErrorCategoryInference.cs` — the transitional rules, deleted by F2.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/ErrorCategoryCompletenessStaticScanTests.cs` and `ErrorCategoryPending.txt` — the completeness guard.

## Known limits

- The inference keeps today's answers, so `encina.request.handler_missing` and `*.missing` still infer `NotFound` until their slices declare `Internal` (R4).
- A code-less error (`EncinaError.New`) is invisible to the scan and is always `Internal`.
- A category passed through a variable is recognised only as a named `category:` argument.
