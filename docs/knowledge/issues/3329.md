## Asked

Stop the shared GraphQL quota helper from acting on a stale cached reading (it skipped 19 project additions on 2026-11-06; the issue reported the real quota as about 3500, read from the stale `gh api rate_limit`), cover a cache whose ResetAt has passed with a self-test, handle the secondary rate limit separately with a back-off, and identify why the stale reading persisted.

## Outcome

`tools/ai/gh-quota.ps1` ignores a cached reading whose `ResetAt` has passed or whose `ReadAtUtc` is in the future, confirms every low cached reading with one live read in `Wait-GhGraphQLQuota` and `Test-GhGraphQLLow`, and adds `Get-GhRateLimitKind` and `Invoke-GhRateLimitBackoff`. `collect-issues.ps1` retries a rate-limited page (the retry loop has no self-test; the helper functions do). The other mutation callers (`project item-add` in `open-remediation.ps1` and `check-project-membership.ps1`) are not yet wired to the back-off.

Root cause (from `artifacts/gh-quota/usage.csv` of 2026-11-06, which is local and not in the repository): the 59 then 0 readings (resetAt 11:25Z) were most likely genuine. 19 `collect-issues` pages ran at 10:38Z and `check-project-membership` ran `project item-list --limit 5000` at 10:39Z and 10:55Z, which cost many points per call, and the project adds only ran at 11:30Z, after the reset, and worked. The "about 3500 remaining" figure in the issue came from `gh api rate_limit`, which this repository already documents as stale. So the stale-cache theory is not proven as the cause of that incident: a 60 s cache cannot explain a block lasting minutes, and `usage.csv` counts calls, not points, so it hid the real cost. What this change fixes are the proven trust gaps (a reading past its `ResetAt`, a reading dated in the future, a low reading believed without a live confirmation). Point-cost accounting and the cost of `item-list --limit 5000` are follow-ups. When a confirming live read fails, `-NoWait` keeps the cached low reading; the waiting path re-reads after 60 s and treats a failed read as unknown, as before.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/gh-quota.ps1` — cache validity rules, live confirmation, rate-limit kind and back-off.
- `tools/ai/gh-quota-selftest.ps1` — the stale, future, low-confirmed and kind cases.
- `tools/ai/priority/collect-issues.ps1` — page retry with the back-off.
