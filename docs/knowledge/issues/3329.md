## Asked

Stop the shared GraphQL quota helper from acting on a stale cached reading (it skipped 19 project additions on 2026-11-06 while the real quota was about 3500), cover a cache whose ResetAt has passed with a self-test, handle the secondary rate limit separately with a back-off, and identify why the stale reading persisted.

## Outcome

`tools/ai/gh-quota.ps1` ignores a cached reading whose `ResetAt` has passed or whose `ReadAtUtc` is in the future, confirms every low cached reading with one live read in `Wait-GhGraphQLQuota` and `Test-GhGraphQLLow`, and adds `Get-GhRateLimitKind` and `Invoke-GhRateLimitBackoff`. `collect-issues.ps1` retries a rate-limited page. The self-test covers each case.

Root cause: the 2026-11-06 reading (59, then 0 at 11:02Z with resetAt 11:25Z) was a genuine in-window reading that the helper kept trusting for up to 60 s with no check of `ResetAt` and no live confirmation. The helper trusted a cached low number as proof and a failed live read as "unknown, proceed"; neither is recoverable from the files alone, so the fix removes both trust paths instead of guessing a single writer.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/gh-quota.ps1` — cache validity rules, live confirmation, rate-limit kind and back-off.
- `tools/ai/gh-quota-selftest.ps1` — the stale, future, low-confirmed and kind cases.
- `tools/ai/priority/collect-issues.ps1` — page retry with the back-off.
