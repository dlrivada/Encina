## Asked

Split five core sharding methods (complexity 12 to 34) into private helpers so that every method the C4 slice of #3419 touches has CRAP <= 10, without changing behavior.

## Outcome

The five methods and every extracted helper have complexity 8 or less with full line coverage, and the local crap-gate reports no changed method above 10. Characterization tests for all five methods came first, in their own commit, and pass before and after the extraction. No public API, error code, message or ErrorCategory changed.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Sharding/ReferenceTables/`, `Execution/ShardedQueryExecutor.cs`, `Migrations/ShardedMigrationCoordinator.cs`, `CompoundShardKeyExtractor.cs` - the refactored methods.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/Sharding/` and `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Migrations/ShardedMigrationCoordinatorTests.cs` - the characterization tests.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Support/ActivityCapture.cs` - parallel-safe activity capture for tests.
