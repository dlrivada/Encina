## Asked

Apply the maintainer decision C' of the transitive pinning spike (#1051): turn central transitive pinning on only for non-packable projects, raise the central versions that conflict, and remove the references that the pin makes redundant.

## Outcome

A root `Directory.Build.targets` sets the flag when the final `IsPackable` is false; `tests/Directory.Build.targets` imports it. Central versions were raised (Grpc 2.80.0, Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience and Resilience 10.8.0, HotChocolate 15.1.14, ArchUnitNET 0.13.3, Verify 31.15.0, Spectre.Console 0.57.2, System.CommandLine 2.0.7), the unreferenced `Grpc.Tools` pin was deleted, and the WireMock OTLP, NBomber MessagePack and four Humanizer references were removed. The nuspec comparison against origin/main changed only the dependency floors of Encina.gRPC, Encina.Extensions.Resilience and Encina.Testing.Verify.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `Directory.Build.targets` — the conditional flag.
- `tests/Directory.Build.targets` — the chained import.
- `docs/infrastructure/cpm-transitive-pinning.md` — the analysis and the "Implemented" note.
