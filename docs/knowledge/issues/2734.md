## Asked

Find why the usage ledger had no rows from 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-22 and make every spawn leave a row in one durable ledger without relying on the agent.

## Outcome

Cause: the ledger was a prompt convention. Workers appended to a copy inside their own worktree (lost when it was removed) and lane sessions did not record. A SubagentStop hook now records every subagent in the main checkout's ledger. The remainder: `tools/ai/agent-usage-reconstruct.ps1` rebuilt the rows of 2026-10-11..22 from the subagent transcripts and its `-CheckDays` mode warns about a day with transcript activity and no ledger row. The main session's own usage is not a subagent and stays unrecorded.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/record-agent-usage.ps1` — the recorder, wired as SubagentStop in `.claude/settings.json`, tested in `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1`.
- `.claude/agents/README.md` — how the ledger is written now.
- `tools/ai/agent-usage-reconstruct.ps1` — reconstruction from transcripts and the missing-day check; `tools/ai/agent-usage-selftest.ps1` is its self-test.
