## Asked

Find why the usage ledger had no rows from 2026-10-11 to 2026-10-22 and make every spawn leave a row in one durable ledger without relying on the agent.

## Outcome

Cause: the ledger was a prompt convention. Workers appended to a copy inside their own worktree (lost when it was removed) and lane sessions did not record. A SubagentStop hook now records every subagent in the main checkout's ledger. Not done here: reconstructing 2026-10-11..22 and the missing-day check; the main session's own usage is not a subagent and stays unrecorded.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/record-agent-usage.ps1` — the recorder, wired as SubagentStop in `.claude/settings.json`, tested in `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1`.
- `.claude/agents/README.md` — how the ledger is written now.
