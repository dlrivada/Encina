## Asked

Audit #16's remediation stage kept a bug-kind draft's invented Related Issues references across two re-runs
(verification passes 4 and 5, 2026-09-27): `Limit-RelatedIssues` (`tools/ai/audit/_remediation-checks.ps1`) only
recognized a `## Related Issues` H2 header or a `- **Related Issues**:` bold bullet as a section start.
`bug_report.md` has neither -- its last section is `## Additional Context`, with no structural marker at all --
so the local model wrote a plain `Related Issues:` line there for the real `16-code-5` draft, which
`Limit-RelatedIssues` did not recognize at all, returning the draft unchanged and leaving `#699`, `#696` and
`#181` (real, open, but unrelated issues) in place.

## Outcome

Delivered: `Limit-RelatedIssues` recognizes a third section-start convention -- a plain `Related Issues:`/
`Related issues` line, case-insensitive, with an optional trailing colon and optional bold markers -- ending at
the next `## ` header or at a blank line not immediately followed by another bullet. For a draft routed to
`bug_report.md` specifically, it additionally sanitizes every `#n` reference found anywhere under the draft's
whole `## Additional Context` section, not only inside a labelled subsection, since that template's only
guidance is to put "related issues" there with no promised structure. `audit-draft-remediation.ps1`'s call site
passes the new `$IsBugReportDraft` flag from the routed template. `Add-RelatedIssuesLine` (the companion
function that inserts a rejected duplicate's note into the section) was widened to the same plain-line form
too, after an adversarial review found it still only matched the two older forms and would have reintroduced
the pre-#1400 detached-note placement bug for a plain-line draft. `Test-Hooks.ps1` covers every form directly
with plain strings, including the real `16-code-5` `Additional Context` text copied into a new fixture, and
every pre-existing `#1400`/`#1424` case still passes.
