## Asked

Register the operation audit store for ADO.NET x3, Dapper x3 and MongoDB, ship the DDL of its table for the six relational providers, and (owner comment) apply ADR-036's rename to the operation store in the same change.

## Outcome

Delivered: every ADR-036 operation-audit name is applied across `src/`, `tests/`, the coverage manifests and the documentation; the seven providers register `IOperationAuditStore` under `UseOperationAuditStore`; the `028` DDL script exists in the six relational packages and in their `000_CreateAllTables.sql`; the integration tests of the nine providers create the table from the shipped script. No operation store reads the clock, so no `TimeProvider` was needed in the stores (the retention services already take it).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/architecture/adr/036-three-audit-stores.md` — the names and the order of work.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/*/OperationAuditStoreRegistrationTests.cs` — registration in both orders, with `ValidateOnBuild` and `ValidateScopes`, and the application-store-wins case.
- `docs/features/audit-tracking.md` — the setup, the DDL and the flag.
