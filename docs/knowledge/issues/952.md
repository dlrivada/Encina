## Asked

Audit `Encina.Marten.GDPR` and `Encina.Marten` for Marten projections whose `Create` methods take `IServiceProvider`, the bug class found in #949, and fix every occurrence.

## Outcome

Audited, no occurrences; architecture test added. Findings on origin/main (3808c2888):

- `Encina.Marten.GDPR` contains no projection.
- The only JasperFx `EventProjection` subclasses in `src/` are `OperationAuditEntryProjection` and `ReadAuditEntryProjection` in `Encina.Audit.Marten`; neither takes `IServiceProvider` (the #949 fix).
- The twelve `*Projection` classes of the compliance packages implement Encina's own `IProjection<>` and do not reference `IServiceProvider`.
- `InlineProjectionDispatcher` and `MartenProjectionManager` in `Encina.Marten` take `IServiceProvider` in their constructors, but they are Encina services, not Marten projections.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Testing/Architecture/ProjectionsNeverTakeServiceProviderTests.cs` — scans all shipped `Encina*` assemblies; falsifiable through test-local projections with an `IServiceProvider` parameter.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/AuditMarten/ConfigureMartenOperationAuditProjectionsTests.cs` — the #949 registration regression test.
