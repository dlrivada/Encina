## Asked

Give the label `p0-mandatory` a bonus in the Priority ranking so such issues rank above ordinary ones.

## Outcome

`Get-PriorityP0Bonus` and `Get-PriorityLabelBonus` in `tools/ai/priority/_priority-lib.ps1` return 50 for `p0-mandatory` and the larger of the urgent and p0 bonuses overall. `New-ScoredEntry` in `score-issues.ps1` adds it, sets the flag `p0-mandatory` and the field `p0Mandatory`, and keeps `urgent`, `baseTotal` and the other fields. SPEC-004 REQ-010 states the rule.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/priority/_priority-lib.ps1` - the pure bonus functions.
- `tools/ai/priority/score-issues.ps1` - the entry fields and header paragraph.
- `tools/ai/priority/Test-Priority.ps1` - the self-test.
- `docs/specifications/SPEC-004-bounded-wip-stage-pipeline.md` - REQ-010.
