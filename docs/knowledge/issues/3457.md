## Asked

Correct the two passages of `docs/features/audit-marten.md` that say a paused shard does not retry ("What happens" and the `KeyMissing` note after the reason table), distinguishing the standalone daemon from the host daemon (`AddAsyncDaemon`, `Solo` or `HotCold`), and cite the measurements of spike #3026 (PR #3349, section 3.1).

## Outcome

"What happens" now states both behaviours, explains that a host daemon retries on every health check after `AgentPauseTime` at the `HealthCheckPollingTime` interval (both store-wide settings on `StoreOptions.Projections`), and gives the measured attempts, intervals and recovery with a link to the spike report. The `KeyMissing` note no longer says the daemon does not retry: a standalone daemon does not, a host daemon retries without success, and in both cases the shard does not move past the event until the cause is repaired.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/features/audit-marten.md` — "Recover from an unreadable audit event", sections "What happens" and "Recognise it".
- `docs/engineering/paused-shard-spike-3026.md` — section 3.1 (measurements) and section 8, D2 (the open decision).

## Known limits

- The page recommends no `AgentPauseTime` value; that waits for decision D2 of spike #3026.
- "Recognise it" and "Repair the cause and rebuild" still show the standalone daemon (`BuildProjectionDaemonAsync`), the topology the integration test uses. "Repair the cause and rebuild" now notes the measured interaction with a host daemon (fenced only by the progression check; `ProgressionProgressOutOfOrderException` in spike section 3.3); the supported recovery path for a host daemon waits for D4 and D10 of spike #3026.
