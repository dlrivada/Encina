## Asked

Make every error the messaging patterns create declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the four `src/Encina.Messaging/` M1 lines leave the completeness scan's pending file.

## Outcome

All 43 calls in 8 files carry a category and the four pending lines are deleted (7 M2 entries remain). The nine `*.cancelled` calls answer 499, `saga.invalid_status` 409, `scattergather.timeout` 504 and `dlq.source_message_id_required` 400; `saga.not_found` stays 404. The two aggregate scatter-gather codes use one internal helper (`ScatterGatherFailureCategory`) at five call sites. Two errors embedded in dead letter records and two unreachable `Right` branches cannot be asserted through a public result: the static scan is their check.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Messaging/ContentRouter/`, `RoutingSlip/`, `Sagas/`, `ScatterGather/` - the categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/MessagingPatternsErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
