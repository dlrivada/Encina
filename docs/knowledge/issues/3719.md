## Asked

Make every error the routing, shadow and time-based sharding code creates declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the three C3 lines can leave the completeness scan's pending file.

## Outcome

All 26 calls in 7 files carry a literal `ErrorCategory`: 25 are `Internal` (group A decided 2026-11-20, D1) and the one `ShardReadOnly` call is `Conflict` (D14, decided 2026-11-20). The three `=> C3` pending lines are deleted (eight sharding entries remain for C4 and C5). The only status change is `encina.sharding.shard_read_only`, 500 to 409.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Sharding/Routing/`, `Shadow/`, `TimeBased/` - the 26 categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/Sharding/ShardingErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
