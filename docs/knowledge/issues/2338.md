## Asked

Make `dotnet restore Encina.slnx` succeed again with NuGet audit on, after advisory GHSA-qhrr-8q5h-9q3h made the transitive `MessagePack` 2.5.302 fail restore as NU1902. The package reached the build through `Aspire.Hosting.Testing` 13.5.4 -> `StreamJsonRpc` 2.25.29 -> `MessagePack` 2.5.302.

## Outcome

`MessagePack` is pinned centrally at 2.5.307 (the latest 2.5.x patch, which also clears GHSA-qhrr-8q5h-9q3h) in `Directory.Packages.props` and referenced directly by `src/Encina.Aspire.Testing` and `tests/Encina.NBomber`, the projects that bring it in through the Aspire and NBomber chains.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `Directory.Packages.props` - the central version of `MessagePack`.
- `src/Encina.Aspire.Testing/Encina.Aspire.Testing.csproj` - direct `PackageReference` to `MessagePack`.
- `tests/Encina.NBomber/Encina.NBomber.csproj` - direct `PackageReference` to `MessagePack`.
