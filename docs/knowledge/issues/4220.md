## Asked

Make the two `RecoverabilityOptionsCompositionTests` tests that fail on .NET 11 with "A circular dependency was detected for the service of type 'Encina.Recoverability.IErrorClassifier'" pass on SDK 10 and on SDK 11, with the same observable behavior on both, ahead of the net11.0 flip (#3667).

## Outcome

The root cause is the unsupported re-entrant pattern: a `RecoverabilityOptions` configure action that injects `IErrorClassifier` re-enters `OptionsClassifierFactory.Create`. .NET 10 re-entered the factory and its guard answered from the earlier registration; .NET 11 throws before the factory runs again. The guard now throws the same circular-dependency `InvalidOperationException`, and the two tests assert that exception on both runtimes.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Messaging/Recoverability/RecoverabilityServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` — `OptionsClassifierFactory.Create` and the remarks of `AddEncinaRecoverability`.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/Recoverability/RecoverabilityOptionsCompositionTests.cs` — the two `ConfigureAction_InjectingTheClassifier_*_ThrowsACircularDependency` tests.
