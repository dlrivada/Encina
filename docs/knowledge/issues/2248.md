## Asked

Make `AddEncinaRecoverability()` and `UseRecoverability` build with defaults, and fail clearly at startup when delayed retries are enabled without a delayed retry store.

## Outcome

Delivered in `Encina.Messaging`: delayed retries default to off, one shared registration, an interim startup check, a clear failure without a host, and the processor's silent branch removed. Persistent stores are #594, #595 and #2249; EF Core wiring is #2711.

- `src/Encina.Messaging/Recoverability/DelayedRetryStartupCheck.cs` - the startup check and message.
- `docs/architecture/adr/050-modules-may-require-modules.md` - the amended "Delayed retries" row.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/Recoverability/DelayedRetryRegistrationTests.cs` - the order and option matrix.
