## Asked

Make every error the messaging infrastructure creates declare its `ErrorCategory`. The issue is split in two pull requests: M2a (dead letter, inbox, outbox, recoverability) and M2b (scheduling, `TransactionPipelineBehavior`, transactions). This record covers M2a; the M2b pending lines stay in the completeness scan's pending file.

## Outcome

All 20 calls in 8 files carry a category and the four M2a pending lines are deleted. Five HTTP statuses change: `encina.authorization.dlq_tenant_required` answers 400 instead of 403, `dlq.already_replayed` and `dlq.expired` answer 409 instead of 500, `dlq.delete_failed` answers 404 instead of 500 and `inbox.missing_message_id` answers 400 instead of 500. The other calls stay `Internal` (500). The XML doc of `DeadLetterErrorCodes.TenantRequired` no longer says 403. Four errors cannot be asserted through a public result (`outbox.unknown_notification_type`, the variable-code error of `OutboxBatchProcessor`, the `inbox.processing_failed` error of the inbox dead letter capture, `recoverability.schedule_retry_failed`): the static scan is their check.

- `src/Encina.Messaging/DeadLetter/`, `Inbox/`, `Outbox/`, `Recoverability/` - the categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/MessagingInfrastructureErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each reachable producer.
