## Asked

Move the EventId range `Core` from `100-199` to `10000-10099` with every literal id of the range, in one pull request and with no change of behavior.

## Outcome

The range and its 77 literal ids moved together (a range is an atom for `EncinaEventIdAllocationTests` and `EventIdUniquenessRule`). Comments, package READMEs, feature pages and the registry cite the new numbers; plans and knowledge records keep the old ones and the registry's migration map translates them.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Diagnostics/EventIdRanges.cs` — the registered range.
- `docs/engineering/eventid-block-registry.md` — registered row, block status and the migration map.
