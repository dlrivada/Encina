## Asked

Make every error `Encina.MongoDB` creates declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the MongoDB line of the completeness scan's pending file can be deleted.

## Outcome

All 17 calls in 8 files carry `ErrorCategory.Internal` and the pending line is deleted; no status changes. `MissingColumnMappings` in `TenantEntityMappingBuilder.Build` cannot be reached through the public API, because `HasId` always adds a field mapping; the static scan is its check. The code-less `EncinaError.New` of `ReadWriteMongoCollectionFactory` stays `Internal` by the 500 fallback (ADR-062 "Known limit"). The inbox not-found codes are held at `Internal` pending decision D10.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.MongoDB/` - the categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/MongoDB/MongoErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each reachable producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
