## Asked

`audit-draft-remediation.ps1 -DryRun` ran its cleanup and its stage-file write regardless of the flag: on
2026-09-28 a dry run during the review of PR #1534 deleted audit #18's 21 real drafts and overwrote the live
`stages/remediation.md` with preview lines.

## Outcome

Delivered by #1572's rewrite of the script: a dry run is a self-contained sandbox under
`artifacts/knowledge/remediation/_dryrun-<n>/`. A Test-Hooks fixture hashes every live draft, input, manifest
and the stage file, runs `-Prepare -DryRun`, `-Finalize -DryRun` and `-Prepare -DryRun -Only`, and proves the
live files are byte-identical afterwards.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/audit/audit-draft-remediation.ps1` -- the sandboxed `-DryRun` of both modes.
- `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1` -- the `#1540` dry-run isolation cases.
