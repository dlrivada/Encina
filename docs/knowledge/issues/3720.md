## Asked

Make every error the core sharding infrastructure creates declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the seven C4 lines can leave the completeness scan's pending file.

## Outcome

All 35 calls in 9 files carry a literal `ErrorCategory`: 31 are `Internal`, 2 `NotFound` (`MigrationNotFound`) and 2 `Timeout` (`ScatterGatherTimeout`, `MigrationTimeout`). The seven `=> C4` pending lines are deleted (`Resharding/ => C5` and the EF2 line remain). Four calls change HTTP status: `migration_not_found` 500 to 404 (2 calls), `scatter_gather_timeout` and `migration_timeout` 500 to 504. D10 and D13 are pending the maintainer's decision and are declared `Internal` meanwhile. The `ShardNotFound` code on the failed shard query of `ShardedQueryExecutor` stays (#4080 recodes it in its own pull request).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Sharding/` (Colocation, Execution, Migrations, ReferenceTables, key extractors, topology) - the 35 categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/Sharding/ShardingInfrastructureErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
