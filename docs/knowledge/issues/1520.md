## Asked

The link check workflow rejects `digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu` with a 403 status code, even though the page is
online and accessible. This blocks CI. The domain is part of the European Commission infrastructure and applies
the same blocking pattern to automated clients that other EU sites (ENISA, EDPB, EUR-Lex) already excluded in
lychee.

## Outcome

Delivered: `digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu` is now excluded from lychee's link check in `.github/lychee.toml`,
alongside `enisa.europa.eu`, `edpb.europa.eu`, and `eur-lex.europa.eu`. The exclusion follows the existing
comment pattern naming the reason and the issue number.
