## Asked

Audit #16 reached verification pass 7 = PASS on 2026-09-27, but `tools/ai/audit/audit-done.ps1` refused to close
it because the knowledge record `artifacts/knowledge/issues/16.md` (written by the archivist stage on
2026-09-25, in an ad hoc format lacking `schema`, `nav_exclude`, `closed`, `state_reason`, `area`, `review`,
`prs`, `knowledge`, `remediation`, `audit`, and using flow-style syntax for `packages`/`linked_prs`) failed
`knowledge-records --check` with 12 errors. `audit-stage-guard.ps1` refused to re-spawn `issue-archivist` to fix
it, since every stage's artifact was already committed and the last verdict was not FAIL -- a genuine deadlock:
`audit-done` refuses, the guard forbids the fix, and the orchestrator cannot hand-edit the record either
(path ownership assigns it to `issue-archivist`).

## Outcome

Delivered: `audit-commit-stage.ps1 -Stage archivist` now runs `knowledge-records --check` on the record before
committing and refuses (printing the validator's output) when it fails, catching the defect at its source
instead of two stages later. When `audit-done.ps1`'s own check fails, it writes
`artifacts/knowledge/stages/.rerun-archivist` (the validator output plus a UTC timestamp) and exits 1 naming
it. `audit-stage-guard.ps1` allows re-spawning `issue-archivist` (only that agent, not another stage) while the
marker exists, in addition to its pre-existing FAIL-verdict exception; a successful archivist re-commit deletes
the marker. `audit-done.ps1` may then run directly -- a record-format fix does not require a verifier re-run,
since the verifier checks the stages' claims, not the record's front-matter shape. `issue-archivist.md`'s Output
section now names schema-1 explicitly, points at `docs/knowledge/issues/1345.md` as a worked example, and tells
the agent to self-check with `knowledge-records --check` before finishing.
