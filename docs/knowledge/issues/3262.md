## Asked

Make the two recoverability registration paths (`AddEncinaRecoverability` and the messaging `UseRecoverability`) share one `RecoverabilityOptions` value, so they can no longer disagree about delayed retries. The error for a directly registered options instance and the duplicate behavior registration are #3263.

## Outcome

Delivered: both paths call `Configure<RecoverabilityOptions>`; the behavior, the processor and the startup check read `IOptions<RecoverabilityOptions>`. The scheduler, processor and startup check are always registered and decide from the resolved value. The custom classifier is chosen at resolution. `UseRecoverability` copies all its settings over the value, so the later registration wins for the settings both set.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Messaging/Recoverability/RecoverabilityServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` - `Configure`, the always-registered delayed-retry pieces and the resolution-time classifier.
- `src/Encina.Messaging/MessagingServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` - `RegisterRecoverability` composes the configured options.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/Recoverability/RecoverabilityOptionsCompositionTests.cs` - both orders, flag on and off, with and without a store, host start and the classifier.
