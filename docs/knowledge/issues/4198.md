## Asked

Make every error the scheduling producers, `TransactionPipelineBehavior` and `TransactionErrors` create declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the three M2b lines of `src/Encina.Messaging/` leave the completeness scan's pending file. This issue is the M2b slice of #3724; its decision register is `artifacts/decisions/3724.md` and its decisions apply.

## Outcome

All 14 calls in 4 files carry a category and the three pending lines are deleted (the M2a lines and `Encina.Messaging.Encryption` remain). `scheduling.invalid_schedule_time` and `scheduling.invalid_delay` answer 400 instead of 500; the other 12 calls now declare `Internal` (500, as the register records for them). The variable-code error embedded in the dead letter record of `SchedulerOrchestrator` cannot be asserted through a public result: the static scan is its check.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Messaging/Scheduling/`, `TransactionPipelineBehavior.cs`, `Transactions/TransactionErrors.cs` - the categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Messaging/MessagingSchedulingTransactionsErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
