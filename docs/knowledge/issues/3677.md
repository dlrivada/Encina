## Asked

Stop `EncinaHub` from choosing the CLR type to execute from a client-supplied name: a host allow-list with a deny-all default, the lookup before deserialization, no assembly scan or unbounded cache, one indistinguishable client error, bounded log output, and a fail-closed authentication gate.

## Outcome

Delivered: `SignalROptions.AllowRequest` / `AllowNotification` build a frozen allow-list keyed by exact `Type.FullName`; the hub's `ResolveType` scan is gone; denials share one `type_not_found` response and a sanitized Debug log; `SignalRAllowListStartupCheck` logs anonymous opt-outs and warns on an empty list with a mapped hub.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.SignalR/SignalROptions.cs` — registration, kind validation, duplicate and freeze rules.
- `src/Encina.SignalR/MediatorHub.cs` — lookup before deserialization and the authentication gate.
- `src/Encina.SignalR/HubTypeRegistration.cs` — the allow-list entry and `ClientTypeName.ForLog`.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/SignalR/EncinaHubAllowListTests.cs` — the acceptance tests of #3677.
