## Asked

Make every error the core pipeline creates declare its `ErrorCategory`, so the pipeline's pending line can leave the completeness scan.

## Outcome

All 22 calls in the 11 files under `src/Encina/Pipeline/` carry a literal category (13 `Internal`, 9 `Cancelled`) and the pending-file line `src/Encina/Pipeline/ => C1` is deleted. A missing handler answers 500 instead of 404 and the nine cancellation codes answer 499 instead of 500.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Pipeline/` - the 22 categorized calls.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/PipelineErrorCategoryTests.cs` - the category of each producer.
- `docs/architecture/adr/062-error-category-contract.md` - the contract.
