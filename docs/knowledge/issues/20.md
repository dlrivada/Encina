## Asked

Consolidate the code duplicated across the Dapper and ADO providers (`Log.cs`, `TransactionPipelineBehavior.cs`, DI registration) into `Encina.Messaging`, leaving the dialect-specific SQL in the providers.

## Outcome

Delivered by commits `760e27c`, `e1c68db` and `19f2402` (closed 2025-12-24 by the maintainer's comment); no pull request. Both shared files exist today in `src/Encina.Messaging/`, and the six surviving providers use them. The Oracle and SQLite share of the consolidation is moot (ADR-009, ADR-024).

## Where the knowledge lives

- The shared-logic and provider-specific split is in `src/Encina.Messaging/`; `AGENTS.md` section 3 ("Provider coherence") states only the general principle, and the specific split is to be documented by #1336 (open).
- The S2077 suppression lives in `.github/workflows/sonarcloud.yml`, scoped per package (#1341, open).
- The accepted store duplication has no ADR yet (#1336, open).

## Audit

Verdict: `findings-tracked`. Three remediation issues: #1340 (six near-identical `TransactionPipelineBehavior` guard test files), #1341 (package-wide S2077 suppression) and #1342 (no logging, tracing or DI test on the shared behavior); the duplication-percentage claim joins #1335. See [docs/knowledge/audits/issue-20.md](../audits/issue-20.md).
