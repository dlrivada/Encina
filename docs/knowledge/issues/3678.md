## Asked

Stop `SignalRNotificationBroadcaster` from sending a `[BroadcastToSignalR]` notification with no targets to `Clients.All`: broadcast to everyone becomes an explicit host registration, and every other targetless or empty-target publish is refused visibly.

## Outcome

Delivered: `SignalROptions.AllowBroadcastToAll<T>()` / `AllowBroadcastToAll(Type)` register the types that may reach every client, frozen together with the hub allow-list; `SelectClients` returns no audience for undeclared targets without registration and for declared targets that resolve empty, and the broadcaster then sends nothing and logs EventId 4614; the attribute example names its targets.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.SignalR/SignalROptions.cs` — the registration, its validation and the shared freeze.
- `src/Encina.SignalR/SignalRNotificationBroadcaster.cs` — audience selection and the refusal.
- `src/Encina.SignalR/Log.cs` — EventId 4614.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/SignalR/SignalRNotificationBroadcasterTests.cs` — the acceptance tests of #3678.
