## Asked

Make `AddEncinaEntityFrameworkCore` honour the recoverability, routing slip, content router and scatter-gather flags like the ADO.NET and Dapper registrations, in an order that keeps retries outside the transaction.

## Outcome

Delivered: the four registration helpers of `Encina.Messaging` are internal and called from the EF registration, the stale remark of `AddOutboxInboxSagaSchedulingServices` is rewritten, and EF soft delete stays the interceptor plus query filter.

- `src/Encina.EntityFrameworkCore/ServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` - the calls and the soft delete comment.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/EntityFrameworkCore/ServiceCollectionExtensionsRecoverabilityRoutersTests.cs` - flags, order, host start and soft delete absence.
