## Asked

`IProcessorService` ignored the tenant: `GetProcessorAsync`, `GetAllProcessorsAsync`, the sub-processor queries and the by-id mutations read and acted on the processors of every tenant (SPEC-002 AC-043). This sub-issue of #3517 adds the tenant foundation that the DPA service (#3680) and the scheduler and health check (#3681) reuse, and scopes the processor service.

## Outcome

`ProcessorAgreementTenantScope` (internal) resolves the tenant for reads and writes; `DefaultProcessorService` filters every query on it, refuses foreign records as not found, checks the sub-processor parent, scopes its cache keys and propagates a store error from the chain traversal. `ProcessorAgreementOptions.RequireTenantContext`, `ProcessorAgreementErrors.TenantRequired`, EventIds 9486-9487, the `Encina.Tenancy` reference and the `IRequestContextAccessor` registration complete the foundation.

For the maintainer: processors stored without a tenant before this change are not visible to tenanted callers, and there is no migration.
