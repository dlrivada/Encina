## Asked

Split statements at a single background `&`, so a write after it (`Set-Content a.txt y & Set-Content src\x.txt y`) is seen by the write guard.

## Outcome

Cause: the tokenizer treated a lone `&` as part of a word, so the whole line was one statement and the targets after it were never judged. `Split-CommandStatements` now ends the statement at a background `&` (`Test-BackgroundAmpersand`) and keeps `&&`, redirections and the leading call operator as before. Fixed together with #3984.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/_command-text.ps1` — `Test-BackgroundAmpersand` and its use in the statement and token loops.
- `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1` — the tokenizer and guard-orchestrator-writes cases (#4056).
