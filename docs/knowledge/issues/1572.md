## Asked

Replace the local model in the SPEC-003 remediation stage with a Claude agent, `remediation-drafter`, after
audit #18 failed verification on local-model drafts that contradicted their findings, and split
`audit-draft-remediation.ps1` into deterministic `-Prepare` and `-Finalize` steps around the agent. Fold in
#1540 (a dry run destroyed live drafts) and #1548 (a transient `gh` failure aborted the stage).

## Outcome

Delivered. `-Prepare` writes the inputs and a manifest; the orchestrator spawns `remediation-drafter`, which
writes each draft and `stages/remediation.md`; `-Finalize` sanitizes and checks the drafts and exits 1 with
every problem, so the drafter is re-spawned until it is clean. `pipeline.json`, `audit-stage-guard.ps1`,
`block-worker-spawn.ps1`, `enforce-path-ownership.ps1` and `audit-commit-stage.ps1` recognise the new agent;
the `issue-audit` skill, the agents README, SPEC-003 and `ai-task-routing.md` describe the new stage.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/audit/audit-draft-remediation.ps1` -- the `-Prepare`/`-Finalize` modes.
- `tools/ai/audit/_remediation-checks.ps1` -- the deterministic helpers, `Get-DraftHeader`,
  `Find-MissingTemplateHeaders` and the `gh` retry helper.
- `.claude/agents/remediation-drafter.md` -- the drafting rules.
- `.claude/hooks/enforce-path-ownership.ps1`, `.claude/hooks/audit-stage-guard.ps1`,
  `.claude/hooks/block-worker-spawn.ps1` -- the enforcement.
- `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1` -- fixture tests for Prepare, Finalize, dry-run isolation, `gh` retry and
  the hooks.
- `.claude/skills/issue-audit/SKILL.md` -- the stage flow.
