## Asked

Issues were opened without a milestone, a priority label, a project or their relationships, so they dropped out of the planning views. Enforce a complete issue in the `check-issue-template` hook, in `open-remediation.ps1` and in a weekly hygiene workflow.

## Outcome

The hook denies an incomplete `gh issue create` and lists every missing item, `open-remediation.ps1` routes milestone and priority and adds each issue to project 1, the `open-issue` and `worker-brief` skills carry the checklist and the `needs-decision` rule, and `issue-hygiene.yml` labels and reports incomplete issues.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/check-issue-template.ps1` — milestone, default label, priority label and `needs-decision` checks.
- `tools/ai/audit/open-remediation.ps1` — routes, project add with the keyring token.
- `.claude/skills/open-issue/SKILL.md` — the full checklist and "Options stay open".
- `.claude/skills/worker-brief/SKILL.md` — no brief for a `needs-decision` issue.
- `.github/workflows/issue-hygiene.yml` — per-event and weekly hygiene, `PROJECT_TOKEN` setup.
