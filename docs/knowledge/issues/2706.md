## Asked

Make `DistributedApplicationTestingBuilderExtensions.WithEncinaTestSupport` (in `src/Encina.Aspire.Testing`) run by real tests: the registration path and the second-call `InvalidOperationException`.

## Outcome

Tests run the method against a hand-written `IDistributedApplicationTestingBuilder` double (`FakeTestingBuilder`) whose `Services` is a real `ServiceCollection` and whose other members throw `NotSupportedException`. They assert the null guard, the options singleton, the configure action, the fake stores, the `EncinaTestContext`, the documented second-call message, and independent builders. The per-file unit and guard targets and justifications in `.github/coverage-manifest/Encina.Aspire.Testing.json` were replaced by measured ones; the measured figures are on the coverage dashboard.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/coverage-manifest/Encina.Aspire.Testing.json` - the per-file unit and guard targets and justifications.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Aspire/Testing/FakeTestingBuilder.cs` and `WithEncinaTestSupportRegistrationTests.cs` - the test double and the registration and second-call tests.
