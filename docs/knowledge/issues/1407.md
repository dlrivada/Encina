## Asked

The `check-links` job failed an unrelated pull request (#1402) on a single transient `503 Service Unavailable` from an external documentation site that had passed the same scan three times in the preceding 24 hours. `.github/lychee.toml` already tolerated `502` for GitHub's own occasional bad responses on issue URLs, but not `429` (rate limiting) or `503` (temporary outage), and `max_retries = 3` retried within seconds, too fast to outlast a short outage.

## Outcome

Delivered: `.github/lychee.toml`'s `accept` list gained `429` and `503` alongside the existing `200, 206, 301, 302, 307, 308, 502`, with a comment block explaining why each of 502/429/503 is transient rather than a broken link. `retry_wait_time = 10` was added next to `max_retries = 3` so the three retries span roughly 30 seconds instead of firing within the same second. Exclude lists, timeouts and `.github/workflows/link-check.yml` were left untouched; `404`/`410`/DNS failures still fail the job.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/lychee.toml` — the widened `accept` list with its reasoning comment, and `retry_wait_time = 10` next to `max_retries`.
