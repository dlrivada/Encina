## Asked

Make `dotnet restore Encina.slnx` and a Release build succeed again after two advisories published on 2026-11-10 (GHSA-f7vh-h5g3-7jrh for `Aspire.Hosting` and `Aspire.Hosting.AppHost` below 13.6.5, GHSA-6527-9fp8-r4x6 for `Microsoft.AspNetCore.WebUtilities` 2.3.0 to 2.3.13) turned NuGet audit warnings into restore errors on every pull request and on `main`.

## Outcome

`Aspire.Hosting.Testing` and `Aspire.Hosting.PostgreSQL` are at 13.6.5, `Microsoft.AspNetCore.WebUtilities` is pinned at 2.3.14, and the central versions that Aspire 13.6.5 requires were raised with them.

- `Directory.Packages.props` - the Aspire versions, the WebUtilities pin with its advisory, and the raised dependent versions.
