## Asked

Close two gaps the #1705 Phase 2 self-review found in the request identity core: an explicit context with a different live identity was accepted over an inbound chain, and a context captured by a pipeline behavior kept its authenticated identity after the issuing scope ended.

## Outcome

Delivered: the explicit-context rule refuses a different authenticated identity over an Inbound or Connection chain fact (EventId 167), and `RequestContext.Identity` reads Anonymous once its issuer has ended, with the identity machinery reading `IssuedIdentity`. The accessor setter also refuses a context whose scope has ended.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Core/AmbientRequestContext.cs` — the explicit-context rule order, including the inbound rule.
- `src/Encina/Core/RequestContext.cs` — the liveness getter and `IssuedIdentity`.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/Identity/InboundExplicitContextAndLivenessTests.cs` — refusals over inbound, connection and External chains; stream and fire-and-forget gates after scope end.
