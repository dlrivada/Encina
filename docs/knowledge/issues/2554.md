## Asked

Split the next heaviest classes of the scheduled `--job medium` benchmark run before they reach the 60-minute job limit.

## Outcome

Hybrid, Memory and Retention are each split into three classes of 2 to 4 cases, and CacheInvalidation into three classes of 2 to 4 cases, over an abstract `*BenchmarkBase` per project. The original class names stay on the first group, the perf manifests list the new classes, and the class-level `[SimpleJob]` attributes are removed.
