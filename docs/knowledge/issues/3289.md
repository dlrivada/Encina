## Asked

Replace the Codecov service with an in-repo comment that reports coverage per flag against the per-file targets, and remove every Codecov reference from workflows, configuration, tooling and living documentation.

## Outcome

Delivered in two parts. Part 1 (PR #3464) added the in-repo coverage comment (ADR-063) and removed the Codecov uploads from the workflows. The comment was confirmed on a real PR (#3348) on 2026-11-10,; removing the Codecov GitHub app and the `CODECOV_TOKEN` secret is a maintainer step after the merge (ADR-063). Part 2 was split into #3478 (the tooling: PR watcher agent, watcher script, self-test, link-check configuration) and #3479 (the documentation pages).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/workflows/coverage-comment.yml` — the trusted job that posts the per-file comment.
- `docs/architecture/adr/063-in-repo-coverage-comment-replaces-codecov.md` — the decision and its security constraints.
- `.claude/agents/pr-watcher.md` — the watcher no longer waits for a Codecov status.
