## Asked

Provide a Wolverine-inspired low-ceremony saga syntax: minimal boilerplate for simple sagas, convention-based routing, inline state and fluent compensation, with the full saga machinery still available for complex cases. Acceptance: 85%+ coverage.

## Outcome

Delivered on 2025-12-26 (commit c13aee3c, `Fixes #41`, no PR): a fluent `SagaDefinition`/`SagaStepBuilder` API and `ISagaRunner`/`SagaRunner` in `Encina.Messaging`, with 45 unit, 11 property and 8 contract tests reported in the closing comment. The `[Saga]` attribute and convention-based routing of the issue body were not built.
