## Asked

Resolve the CA1307 warnings the SDK 11 analyzers report in the MongoDB sharding factories, with a change that stays warning-free on SDK 10 so the later switch to net11.0 needs no rework.

## Outcome

`ExtractDatabaseName` in `ShardedMongoCollectionFactory` and `ShardedReadWriteMongoCollectionFactory` now slices a `ReadOnlySpan<char>` instead of calling `IndexOf(char, int)`; the behavior is unchanged and is pinned by new unit tests.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/MongoDB/Sharding/ShardedFactoryDatabaseNameExtractionTests.cs` - the database-name extraction cases for both factories.
