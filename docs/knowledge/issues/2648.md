## Asked

Raise the unit, guard, contract and property coverage of `Encina.Compliance.Attestation` to the package targets of its coverage manifest, with per-file targets and justifications, without changing production code.

## Outcome

Delivered by tests only: a stub-handler suite for `HttpAttestationProvider`, a support suite (errors, hasher, diagnostics with a listener, receipt retrieval and store members, options, models), extended guard tests, contract tests for `AttestationPipelineBehavior` against the real providers, and FsCheck properties for `HttpAttestationOptionsValidator` (the Attestation part of #2041). The manifest carries per-file targets for every measured file.
