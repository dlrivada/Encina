## Asked

Stop the weekly Benchmarks run from being cancelled by the 60-minute job limit of one class, and stop one failed or cancelled matrix job from blocking publication of the series that succeeded.

## Outcome

`ProcessorAgreementServiceBenchmarks` and `DPIAServiceBenchmarks` are each split into three classes over a shared abstract base, every class with an estimated 15 to 23 minutes of benchmark time. `publish-benchmarks.yml` publishes a partial run when its aggregate job succeeded, with a warning naming the missing jobs.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tests/Encina.BenchmarkTests/Encina.Benchmarks/Compliance/ProcessorAgreements/` and `.../DPIA/` - the split classes and their `*BenchmarkBase.cs`.
- `.github/perf-manifest/Encina.Benchmarks.json` - the class list the matrix reads.
- `.github/workflows/publish-benchmarks.yml` - the relaxed source-run gate.
