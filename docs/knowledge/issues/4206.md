## Asked

Remove the CA1877 warning that SDK 11 reports at `src/Encina.Cli/Services/CodeGenerator.cs` when building for `net11.0`, with a change that also builds warning-free on SDK 10 and keeps the behavior.

## Outcome

`BuildTestProjectsJson` now calls `Path.Combine([root, .. parts])` once, and finds the last `src` segment with `List<T>.FindLastIndex` (which also keeps the method's complexity under the CRAP gate). The derived Stryker test-project path is pinned by `GenerateStrykerConfigAsync_ProjectPathWithSrcSegment_DerivesTestPath`.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Cli/Services/CodeGenerator.cs` - the single `Path.Combine` call in `BuildTestProjectsJson`.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Cli/Services/CodeGeneratorExtendedTests.cs` - pins the produced test-project path.
