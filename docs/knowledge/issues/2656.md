## Asked

Make `HttpAttestationProvider.AttestAsync` and `VerifyAsync` return `Left` or the documented `Right` instead of throwing when an endpoint answers 2xx with an empty, non-JSON, wrongly typed or wrongly shaped body.

## Outcome

The response is parsed through one helper that maps parse failures to constant error text; each field is type-checked before it is read; `AttestAsync` counts the new failures in the failed-attestation metric. The `ex.Message` embedded in transport and timeout texts is out of scope and tracked separately in #2822.

- `src/Encina.Compliance.Attestation/Providers/HttpAttestationProvider.cs` - the parsing helpers and the `VerifyAsync` remarks.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Compliance/Attestation/HttpAttestationProviderTests.cs` - one test per malformed shape.
