## Asked

Remove the CA2026 warning reported at `DebeziumHttpListener.cs:177` on SDK 11 / net11.0 without changing behavior and without breaking the SDK 10 build.

## Outcome

`JsonDocument.Parse(body).RootElement.Clone()` became `JsonElement.Parse(body)`. The request handler was split into small private methods (`IsAuthorized`, `EnqueueEvent`) to keep its CRAP at or below 10, and the listener got its first unit tests.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Cdc.Debezium/DebeziumHttpListener.cs` - the parse and backpressure step.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Cdc/Debezium/DebeziumHttpListenerTests.cs` - status codes 200, 400, 401, 405 and 503.
