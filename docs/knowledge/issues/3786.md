## Asked

Remove `DPIAOptions.EnableExpirationMonitoring` so the DPIA review-reminder service is always registered and runs, with no switch replacing it (the DPIA slice of #3270).

## Outcome

The option, its validator rule, its health-check data entry and its "disabled" log message are gone. `AddEncinaDPIA` always registers `DPIAReviewReminderService` through `TryAddEnumerable`; the service's `PeriodicTimer` runs on the injected `TimeProvider`; `ExpirationCheckInterval` is always validated within the `PeriodicTimer` range.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Compliance.DPIA/ServiceCollectionExtensions.cs` — unconditional registration.
- `src/Encina.Compliance.DPIA/DPIAReviewReminderService.cs` — first cycle at start, injected clock, per-cycle catch.
- `src/Encina.Compliance.DPIA/DPIAOptionsValidator.cs` — interval range check.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Compliance/DPIA/DPIAReviewReminderServiceTests.cs` — always-on, injected clock, failing store, DI graph validation.

Not audited: this record is written with the change itself (SPEC-003 REQ-031).
