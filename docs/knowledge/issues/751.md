## Asked

An opt-in, queryable audit trail of ABAC decisions: who asked for what, which policies decided and why, written before the protected handler runs and failing closed by default.

## Outcome

Phases 1 and 2 of the implementation plan are delivered. Phase 1: the evaluation trace of the policy decision point, the decision record, the enforced-outcome and resource-identity types, the schema constants and the new error factories. Phase 2: the Policy Enforcement Point decides, records write-ahead through `IABACDecisionRecorder` and then enforces, with the `DecisionAudit` options it needs. Phase 3: the durable recorder through `IOperationAuditStore`, the entry mapper with the column-limit rule, the reader with its tenant gate and the JSON Lines export, and the core `MultiTenancyMarker`. The remaining phases follow in separate pull requests. The issue stays open until Phase 8; the `closed`, `state_reason` and `outcome: partial` fields of this record are placeholders the schema requires and are updated when the issue closes.
