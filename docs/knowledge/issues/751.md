## Asked

An opt-in, queryable audit trail of ABAC decisions: who asked for what, which policies decided and why, written before the protected handler runs and failing closed by default.

## Outcome

Phases 1 to 6 of the implementation plan are delivered. Phase 1: the evaluation trace of the policy decision point, the decision record, the enforced-outcome and resource-identity types, the schema constants and the new error factories. Phase 2: the Policy Enforcement Point decides, records write-ahead through `IABACDecisionRecorder` and then enforces, with the `DecisionAudit` options it needs. Phase 3: the durable recorder through `IOperationAuditStore`, the entry mapper with the column-limit rule, the reader with its tenant gate and the JSON Lines export, and the core `MultiTenancyMarker`. Phase 4: the configuration, dependency-injection registration and startup checks of the decision audit. Phase 5: the decision-audit health state reported by the ABAC health check, the proof that a decision row survives the rollback of the surrounding transaction, and one core tenancy marker for every tenant-aware gate (#2106). Phase 6: the counters, histogram, span and decision-id tag of the audit path. Phases 7 and 8: the tests, then the documentation, ADR-047, the changelog fragment and this record. The `closed` date is the date of the Phase 8 pull request and is corrected at the audit if the merge falls on another day.
