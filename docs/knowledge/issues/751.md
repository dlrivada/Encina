## Asked

An opt-in, queryable audit trail of ABAC decisions: who asked for what, which policies decided and why, written before the protected handler runs and failing closed by default.

## Outcome

Phase 1 of the implementation plan is delivered: the evaluation trace of the policy decision point, the decision record, the enforced-outcome and resource-identity types, the schema constants and the new error factories. The enforcement, recording and reading phases follow in separate pull requests. The issue stays open until Phase 8; the `closed`, `state_reason` and `outcome: partial` fields of this record are placeholders the schema requires and are updated when the issue closes.
