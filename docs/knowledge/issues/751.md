## Asked

Record every ABAC authorization decision in a formal audit trail, so an auditor can reconstruct who was allowed or denied what and why.

## Outcome

Delivered in eight phases of the implementation plan `docs/plans/abac-decision-audit-implementation-plan-751.md`: the enforcement point writes one `OperationAuditEntry` per enforced decision through the application's `IOperationAuditStore`, write-ahead and fail-closed by default (ADR-047), with configuration, DI and startup checks, a typed reader with JSON Lines export, cross-cutting integration, observability and documentation.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/architecture/adr/047-abac-decision-audit.md` - the decision record.
- `docs/features/abac/audit-decisions.md` - how to audit decisions and the points to check.
- `docs/features/abac/reference/decision-audit.md` - entry shape, limits, recorder, reader.
- `docs/plans/abac-decision-audit-implementation-plan-751.md` - the plan with the Maintainer Decisions D1-D7 and A1-A9.
