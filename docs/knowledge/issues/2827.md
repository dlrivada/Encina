## Asked

Make a package that has a coverage manifest but no measured lines visible as "not measured" in the report and on the dashboard, warn about it, and give CI a check mode that fails on it.

## Outcome

The report seeds its package list from the manifests, marks such packages `measured: false` and lists them in `unmeasuredPackages`; the dashboard renders a not-measured row and a warning block; `coverage-report.cs --check-unmeasured` exits 1 naming them. The CI wiring is left to #2896.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/scripts/coverage-report.cs` — `SeedPackages`, the warning and `--check-unmeasured`, with `--self-test` checks.
- `docs/coverage/app.js` — the not-measured row and the "Packages Not Measured" block.
- `docs/testing/coverage-measurement-methodology.md` — how to read the dashboard.
