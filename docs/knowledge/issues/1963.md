## Asked

Make the requests and processing restrictions of one tenant unreadable, uncacheable-across-tenants, unmutable and unlistable by another tenant in `Encina.Compliance.DataSubjectRights`, as #1315 did for Consent.

## Outcome

Delivered on branch `fix/1963-dsr-tenant-isolation`: `DefaultDSRService` filters every query by the ambient tenant, reports another tenant's request as not found in `GetRequestAsync` and the six lifecycle commands, keys its cache by tenant, takes the ambient tenant in `SubmitRequestAsync` and `HandleRestrictionAsync`, and honours `DataSubjectRightsOptions.RequireTenantContext`.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Compliance.DataSubjectRights/Services/DefaultDSRService.cs` — tenant scope helpers, query predicates, cache keys.
- `src/Encina.Compliance.DataSubjectRights/ProcessingRestrictionPipelineBehavior.cs` — fails closed on `dsr.tenant_required` in Block mode.
- `tests/Encina.IntegrationTests/Compliance/DataSubjectRights/DSRTenantScopingIntegrationTests.cs` — two tenants against Marten on PostgreSQL.
