## Asked

Delete Encina's duplicated fake clock (`Encina.Testing.Time`) and use Microsoft's `FakeTimeProvider` in every helper and test, fixing the defects of the old clock (#2454, #2746) by removing it.

## Outcome

`src/Encina.Testing/Time/` is gone; the seven helpers expose and register Microsoft's type, `Encina.Testing` depends on `Microsoft.Extensions.TimeProvider.Testing`, the signalling clock moved to `Encina.TestInfrastructure`, and the docs and coverage manifest follow.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `docs/en/guides/TESTING.md` — the "Testing Time" section with the convention.
- `tests/Encina.TestInfrastructure/Time/TimerSignallingTimeProvider.cs` — the shared signalling clock.
