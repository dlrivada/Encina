## Asked

Make `[MaskInLogs]` properties masked on the log path, including `LogInformationMasked` and the other `PIILoggerExtensions` methods, and fail closed wherever the masking cannot apply.

## Outcome

The fix is split in sub-issues. This record covers the scanner and options part (#3029): class-level `[MaskInLogs]` applies to every string property, the always-masked attribute wins over the log-only one at any level, a non-string `[MaskInLogs]` property is marked `Unmaskable` and makes only the log context fail closed, and `PIIOptions.MaskInLogs = false` is a logged opt-out with EventId 8024. Unit, property and contract-level tests use the real masker and scanner.

Out of scope and tracked elsewhere: removing `MaskingMode.Tokenize` (#3031), documentation, benchmark and justification files (#3030), and `[PII]` on non-string properties (#3032).

- `src/Encina.Security.PII/PIIMasker.cs` — the fail-closed branch of the log context.
- `src/Encina.Security.PII/PIIOptionsValidator.cs` — the logged opt-out.
