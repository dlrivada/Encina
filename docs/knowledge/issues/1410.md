## Asked

The orchestrator (main session) drafted the bodies of issues #1407 and #1409 and PR #1408 with paid tokens on 2026-09-27, even though AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md route drafts and first drafts of issue/PR bodies to the free local model through `tools/ai/local-ai-ask.cs`. Nothing enforced this for issue bodies the way the SPEC-003 audit pipeline's own scripts and hooks enforce its own rules; the rule survived only on discipline, and drifted immediately. #1410 asked for `check-issue-template.ps1` to refuse a `gh issue create --body-file` call that carries no evidence of local-model drafting, with a logged, explicit opt-out for the cases the local model genuinely cannot handle.

## Outcome

Delivered: `check-issue-template.ps1` (unchanged file, not a new hook) now also requires, for `--body-file` only, one of a `<!-- local-draft: <path> -->` first-line pointer corroborated by a recent `artifacts/local-ai/ledger.csv` row (the repository root's own or any worktree's), the body file itself being such a recent ledger `outFile`, a content-matched `artifacts/knowledge/remediation/` draft (covering `tools/ai/audit/open-remediation.ps1`, whose real `--body-file` mechanism turned out to differ from the issue's own proposed-solution wording — see the decision above), or a first-line `<!-- local-draft: none, reason: <text> -->` opt-out logged to `artifacts/local-ai/opt-outs.log`. `Test-Hooks.ps1` covers all of it (pointer+ledger accepted, body-is-outFile accepted, opt-out-with-reason accepted and logged, opt-out-without-reason refused, no-evidence refused, stale ledger line refused, pointer with no ledger line refused, the open-remediation content match accepted) against two isolated fake repository roots, plus every pre-existing case still passing. `open-issue`/`local-ai-task` skills document the pointer/opt-out lines for a worker or the orchestrator to use.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/check-issue-template.ps1` — the local-draft evidence functions (`Get-RepoRoot`, `Get-CandidateRoots`, `Get-RecentLedgerRows`, `Test-LedgerOutFileMatch`, `Test-PointerEvidence`, `Test-RemediationDraftMatch`, `Test-OptOut`, `Test-LocalDraftCompliance`) and the refusal message.
- `.claude/hooks/tests/Test-Hooks.ps1` — the `#1410` fixtures (`IssueRepo`, `OptOutRepo`, the ledger/pointer/remediation files) and test cases.
- `.claude/skills/open-issue/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/local-ai-task/SKILL.md` — the pointer/opt-out line documented for whoever creates the issue.
