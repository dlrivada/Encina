## Asked

Encrypt `[CryptoShredded]` properties on nested objects, collection elements, dictionary values and non-public properties the serializer writes, with the key of the subject named by the declaring object's `SubjectIdProperty`, and validate every shape recursively at startup with a reason per property (maintainer option B).

## Outcome

Delivered: the serializer is now a System.Text.Json contract modifier with fail-closed reads, a v2 token, an installation check, erasure routing and a startup validator over the contract graph. `AggregateBase` and `Entity<TId>` gain the serialization attributes that make them owners.

- `src/Encina.Marten.GDPR/Serialization/` — modifier, engine, frames, token and cipher.
