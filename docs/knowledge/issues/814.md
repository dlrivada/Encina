## Asked

Give every refused data subject request a standardised reason code next to the explanation given to the subject (GDPR Art. 12(4) and 12(5)), with the Digital Omnibus ground of abusive intent shipped but off by default.

## Outcome

Delivered by the #814 pull request: `DSRRejectionReason` and its extension block, `DSRRequestDenied` with code and explanation, the gated `EnableOmnibusArticle12RefusalGrounds` option, `dsr.rejection_reason_not_enabled`, the instrumented deny path, and the removal of the unused pre-Marten `DSRRequest` record.

- `src/Encina.Compliance.DataSubjectRights/Services/DefaultDSRService.cs` — input validation and the draft-law gate.
- `docs/plans/dsar-rejection-reasons-implementation-plan-814.md` — design choices and the maintainer decisions of 2026-10-06.
