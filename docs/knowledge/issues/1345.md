Replace the SPEC-003 audit's prose coordinator brief with scripts and hooks that enforce one audit open at a time, a fixed six-stage pipeline with one artifact per stage, single-owner stage agents, and an independent verifier, after batching, cheap paths and background specialists produced a false "implemented" claim on #26.

## Outcome

Delivered through PR #1367: `tools/ai/audit/` (queue and stage scripts), the four stage agents plus dual-mode `docs-reviewer`, the `issue-audit` skill, and the enforcing hooks (`audit-stage-guard.ps1`, `block-worker-spawn.ps1`, `enforce-path-ownership.ps1`, `no-background-specialists.ps1`, `_write-targets.ps1`).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/hooks/audit-stage-guard.ps1` — one audit at a time, fixed stage order from `pipeline.json`, no batching.
- `.claude/hooks/block-worker-spawn.ps1` — stage agents and read-only specialists cannot delegate.
- `.claude/skills/issue-audit/SKILL.md` — the orchestrator's procedure for the pipeline.
- `.claude/hooks/_write-targets.ps1` — sees call-operator and dot-source script launches, closing a replay gap.
