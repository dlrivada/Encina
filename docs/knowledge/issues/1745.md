## Asked

Stop the CI Full `pack` job, and with it the publish to GitHub Packages on a `v*` tag, from running when a test job failed, was skipped or was cancelled.

## Outcome

Delivered: `pack` lists `build` and the six test jobs in `needs:`, drops `always()` and its `if:` requires every result to be `success`. The page work of #1747 and #1748 on `docs/releases/RELEASE-PROCESS.md` is covered by this record and by the one for #1721.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/workflows/ci-full.yml` — the `pack` job `needs:` and `if:`.
- `docs/releases/RELEASE-PROCESS.md` — the pre-tag requirement of a green CI Full run (#1747) and the corrected account of run 35652357800 (#1748).
