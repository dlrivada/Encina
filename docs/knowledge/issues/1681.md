## Asked

Make the aggregate job of Mutation Tests keep, for every file, the entry that has mutants, and fail loudly when the merged mutant count differs from the sum of the shard reports.

## Outcome

The "Merge shard reports" step of `.github/workflows/mutation-tests.yml` filters empty entries before combining, rejects overlapping shards and checks the count. Replayed on the shard reports of run 36959365309, the old expression gave 154 mutants in 6 files and the new one gives 2,728 mutants in 82 files, equal to the shards' sum.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.github/workflows/mutation-tests.yml` — the "Merge shard reports" step in the `aggregate` job.
