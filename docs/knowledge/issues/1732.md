## Asked

Stop the control board depending on the orchestrator's memory: the Audits, In flight and Flow tabs were stale (merged work still shown as running, nothing in Flow from 2026-10-04) and had to be reconciled by hand with 21 writes.

## Outcome

Delivered: `tools/ai/board/reconcile-board.ps1` (deterministic reconciliation into batch files of at most 50 writes), its self-test, the non-blocking `board-event-reminder.ps1` PostToolUse hook with Test-Hooks cases, and `tools/ai/board/README.md` with the scheduled-task contract. The orchestrator creates the 30-minute scheduled task after merge.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `tools/ai/board/reconcile-board.ps1` — the rules (merged, pr-open, running, stopped, new cards, audits, meta status).
- `tools/ai/board/README.md` — the scheduled-task contract and the versions sidecar.
- `.claude/hooks/board-event-reminder.ps1` — the reminder hook.
