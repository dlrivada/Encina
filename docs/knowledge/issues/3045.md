## Asked

Make the two Dapper inbox stores (SqlServer, MySQL) never send more parameters than the provider allows in `RemoveExpiredMessagesAsync`, whatever `InboxOptions.PurgeBatchSize` is (sub-issue of #1969, used by the purge service of #3013).

## Outcome

Delivered: each store deletes in chunks of 1000 ids in order; an empty list returns before opening a connection; PostgreSQL unchanged.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Dapper.SqlServer/Inbox/InboxStoreDapper.cs` - chunked `DELETE`.
- `src/Encina.Dapper.MySQL/Inbox/InboxStoreDapper.cs` - chunked `DELETE`.
- `tests/Encina.IntegrationTests/Dapper/SqlServer/Inbox/InboxStoreDapperTests.cs` and `tests/Encina.IntegrationTests/Dapper/MySQL/Inbox/InboxStoreDapperTests.cs` - 2500 ids over three chunks, survivor rows untouched.
