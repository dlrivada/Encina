## Asked

Make the four SQL Server saga DDL scripts (`003_CreateSagaStatesTable.sql` and `000_CreateAllTables.sql` of `Encina.ADO.SqlServer` and `Encina.Dapper.SqlServer`) hold a saga written by `SagaStoreADO` and `SagaStoreDapper`.

## Outcome

The scripts now declare `Status NVARCHAR(50) NOT NULL`, `TimeoutAtUtc`, `CorrelationId` and `Metadata`, as the PostgreSQL and MySQL scripts do. `SqlServerSchemaScriptsIntegrationTests` builds the schema from each of the four scripts in a database of its own and starts, reads, expires and updates a saga through both stores. The outbox, inbox, scheduled message and dead letter scripts were compared with their stores and match.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.ADO.SqlServer/Scripts/003_CreateSagaStatesTable.sql` and the three sibling scripts: the corrected table.
- `tests/Encina.IntegrationTests/SchemaScripts/SqlServerSchemaScriptsIntegrationTests.cs`: the script-to-store test.
