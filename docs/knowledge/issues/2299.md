## Asked

Make `dotnet restore Encina.slnx` succeed again with NuGet audit on, after two new advisories (GHSA-2fmv-rv2c-59x7, GHSA-846f-f764-76mx) made the transitive `System.Security.Cryptography.Pkcs` 9.0.13 fail every restore as NU1902/NU1903.

## Outcome

`System.Security.Cryptography.Pkcs` is pinned centrally at 10.0.13 (the first patched 10.x release) in `Directory.Packages.props` and referenced directly by the six projects that bring in `Microsoft.Data.SqlClient` or `Oracle.ManagedDataAccess.Core`. `System.Security.Cryptography.Xml` moves to 10.0.13 because its 10.0.12 also carried advisories that failed restore.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `Directory.Packages.props` - the central versions of `System.Security.Cryptography.Pkcs` and `System.Security.Cryptography.Xml`.
