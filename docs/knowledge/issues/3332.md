## Asked

Stop `PIIMasker` from returning an object unmasked when its JSON form is an array, a scalar or `null` (a collection-derived type, a type with a scalar `JsonConverter`) while the type has decorated members, which let personal data reach responses, audit entries and logs.

## Outcome

`MaskViaPropertyMetadata` and `MaskObjectViaJson` now fail closed with `InvalidOperationException` (type, root kind and member names, never a value) when a decorated member applies to the context; the existing catch sites turn it into `pii.masking_failed`, `audit.masking_failed` or `[MASKING FAILED]`. Real masking of such roots remains #1834.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina.Security.PII/PIIMasker.cs` - `TryGetObjectRoot` and `AppliesToContext`.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Security/PII/PIINonObjectRootFailClosedTests.cs` - every entry point, both shapes, the log-only and undecorated cases and the opt-out flags.
