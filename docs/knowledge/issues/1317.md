## Asked

Prove with a unit test that the core `AddEncina()` registration, in both overloads, builds a service graph with `ValidateOnBuild` and `ValidateScopes` enabled and resolves every service it registers.

## Outcome

Delivered as `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/AddEncinaServiceGraphTests.cs`: a test per overload, a stream scanning test (stream handler and open stream pipeline behavior discovered, resolved and executed), and a falsifiability theory that removes each single-instance registration and expects the strict check to fail. The registration itself needed no change.
