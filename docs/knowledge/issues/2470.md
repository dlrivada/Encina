## Asked

Stop forcing a maintainer decision for every Design Choice and every issue that lists options: a triage of 2026-10-17 found 0 real dilemmas among 105 `needs-decision` issues. Extract every implicit choice into a register, classify it, verify the settled ones adversarially and let only real Dilemmas reach the maintainer.

## Outcome

Delivered: the read-only `decision-extractor` agent (with the hook allowlist and write-ownership entries it needs), the Trivial / Settled / Dilemma wording in the `open-issue`, `worker-brief` and `implementation-plan` skills, the plan prompt, the feature and spike templates, `remediation-drafter` and `pr-reviewer`, the `check-plan.ps1` rules with fixtures, and the `check-issue-template.ps1` rule with hook tests. Two acceptance criteria were split out by decision of this PR into follow-up issues instead of being left open: listing Settled register entries in the daily report and on the board for maintainer veto (#2478), and the retroactive adversarial verification of the 105 triage verdicts of 2026-10-17 (#2479; the triage files are kept under `artifacts/decisions/triage-2026-10-17/`).

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/agents/decision-extractor.md` - the register contract and the classes.
- `tools/ai/plans/check-plan.ps1` and `tools/ai/plans/fixtures/` - the plan gate for Settled, Trivial and Dilemma choices.
- `.claude/hooks/check-issue-template.ps1` - `needs-decision` only with a Dilemma in the body.
- `.claude/skills/open-issue/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/worker-brief/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/implementation-plan/SKILL.md` - where the register lives and when the maintainer is asked.
