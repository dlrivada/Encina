## Asked

Move the agents' lessons that existed only in the stash `lessons-local-pending-9nov` and in `artifacts/handoff/lessons-backup-2026-11-09/` into the repository, without duplicating lessons that other sessions had already written to `main`.

## Outcome

The lessons of six agents (audit-verifier, docs-reviewer, issue-archivist, issue-auditor, remediation-drafter, test-auditor) that `main` did not have were appended to their files in `.claude/agents/lessons/`. Lessons already present in the same or other words were skipped, and no source lesson contradicted a later one.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `.claude/agents/lessons/*.md` — the per-agent lessons files read by each agent at start.

## Known limits

- The duplicate check is a reading judgement, not a mechanical one; a lesson that restates an older one with new detail was skipped when its point was already covered.
