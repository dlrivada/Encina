## Asked

Make every `RequestContext` factory honour an injected `TimeProvider` instead of reading the wall clock, as AGENTS.md section 3 requires.

## Outcome

`RequestContext.Create` and `RequestContext.CreateForTest` accept an optional `TimeProvider? timeProvider = null` and stamp `Timestamp` from it; unit tests with `FakeTimeProvider` prove it.

## Where the knowledge lives

- `src/Encina/Core/RequestContext.cs` — the factories and their `timeProvider` parameter.
- `tests/Encina.UnitTests/Core/Identity/RequestContextIdentityTests.cs` — the `FakeTimeProvider` tests.
